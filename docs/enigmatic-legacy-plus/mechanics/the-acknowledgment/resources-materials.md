# Resources &amp; Materials

<small>[EnigmaticLegacy+](../../index.md) &rsaquo; [The Acknowledgment](index.md)</small>

You are perfectly familiar with most mundane materials and resources, which this world holds plenty of. But should you dig a little further - and you might discover things even you have never beheld before.  
  
As you keep seeking new materials to forge better tools and armor from, it would be worth to denote unusual properties of some of them.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/earth_heart.png) Heart of the Earth

Similar to how there is Heart of the Sea, an item bound with nature of endless oceans and seas covering surface of the Overworld, there is Heart of the Earth, born within its rocky depths and encapsulating some of their raw energy. It can be found in deepest rooms and halls of dungeons deep below the Overworld's surface, lost and forgotten in smothering darkness.

**The Heart**

![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/earth_heart.png) [Heart of the Earth](../../items/materials/earth-heart.md)

The elemental power this heart holds bears resemblance to that stored within spellstones, but unlike latter it isn't originated from primordial chaos that spelled the beginning of all worlds, instead being born much later, slowly condensating in the earth's crust during many geological eons.

**The Fragment**

![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/earth_heart_fragment.png) [Fragment of the Earth](../../items/materials/earth-heart-fragment.md)

The resulting shape doesn't attend its bearer and does not allow to freely channel unrefined energy stored within, but may serve as core for powerful relics requiring such energy if utilized properly.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/infernal_cinder.png) Infernal Cinder

The blazing flames of hell have left such ashes scattered throughout the Nether. The cinder still flaunt their wrath, seeking an outlet to vent.
  
You can use the cinder to create many items, but it's obvious that they are all a bit extreme and full of personality. If you can't handle them well, don't try them easily.

**The Cinder**

![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/infernal_cinder.png) [Infernal Cinder](../../items/generic/infernal-cinder.md)

Meanwhile, the compatibility between the cinder and curse is excellent. If you are carrying some curses at the same time, feel free to make bold attempts, as it will not let you down.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/ichor_droplet.png) Ichor Droplet

For this so-called "blood of gods", you have finally discovered a stable source to obtain, which is to hunt for screaming ghasts floating in the Nether.
  
The power of the curse triggered the tiny residual "divinity" in the blood and tears of the ghast, allowing the Ichor to reappear in front of everyone.

**The Ichor**

![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/ichor_droplet.png) [Ichor Droplet](../../items/generic/ichor-droplet.md)

However, do not ponder where these 'divinity' actually comes from, as it has long been a secret of ancient times. For you now, all you need to think about is how to utilize and develop this unexpected product. For example, brewing some extreme potions.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/etherium_ingot.png) The Etherium

Despite the name is has, this material is physically solid. Very.
  
Stories have turned out to be true. And even more - they were not exaggerating the truth. Etherium truly has most of the wondrous properties that were assigned to it by pieces of lore you happened across earlier. It has quite high melting point, being just

as resistant to fire and lava as netherite is, while significantly superseding its durability. Apart from that, this metal is overfilled with pulsing energy, urging you to unleash it in any shape or form you can come up with.
  
And for being alike you, capability to create shape and form is beyond questioning.

*Recipe:* `enigmaticlegacyplus:etherium_ingot_from_blasting`

This world will soon behold the might of your newly forged etherium tools.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/astral_dust.png) Astral Dust

Concealed within the rooms of End Cities, this elusive substance does possess incredibly interesting properties. Power of all elements seems to be infused and mixed within it, somehow holding itself in relatively stable form. Astral Dust resembles creation itself, chaotic and impermanent, yet somehow frozen in time, allowing you to behold all

**Za Dusto**

![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/astral_dust.png) [Astral Dust](../../items/generic/astral-dust.md)

beauty of its single instant.
  
The arcane power that it holds might be immense, but it requires to be carefully treated and molded to be useful in a constructive way. As time goes by, however, there is no doubt you will discover many possible applications it can have.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/cosmic_heart.png) Heart of the Cosmos

With a fine selection of arcane ingredients, those which contain the power of other dimensions, you can alter the earthly nature of Heart of the Sea, transforming it into something different... Vastly more powerful.
  
The power of cosmos itself now rests at the palm of your hand. Only time will tell what unimaginable feats can be

achieved with it, what fabulous artifacts can be powered with its reality-bending energies.
  
But one thing is for certain - the world will tremble beneath the steps of their bearer.

*Recipe:* `enigmaticlegacyplus:cosmic_heart`

## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/twisted_heart.png) Twisted Heart

...and thus, it was you who have chosen this fate. You have surely acknowledged what sorrow and regret this choice will cost you many times in the long journey ahead, but you still have done it. Do you even know why?
  
Regardless, there is no way back - only ahead, in an attempt to harness the power of great curses you are now

bound with. This task is hopefully not as complicated as it might seem if you can gain access to required ingredients, as curses themselves seem to actively seek new ways to spread tendrils of their corruption

*Recipe:* `enigmaticlegacyplus:twisted_heart`

into the world around you - they just require a little assistance from you.
  
As soon as you laid claim upon Heart of the Earth, you have noticed that it resonates with dark power of the ring, setting pulsations of pure energy within it slightly out of tact. This influence is hopefully not strong enough to instantly tarnish the heart, therefore rendering it unusable for regular crafting. But with small amount of carefully selected

arcane ingredients and a fraction of your demigod power involved, you can reinforce the link between the heart's core and curses that you bear, allowing latter to corrupt it and transform into something entirely different.
  
Twisted Heart is a product of this unholy ritual, an artifact incarnating part of the great curse, darker and more powerful than what Heart of the Earth used to contain.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/evil_essence.png) Nefarious Essence

While the Nether Star is a core of Wither's physical vessel, this substance is a manifestation of pure energy of its seemingly indestructible and perpetual soul. A tiny fraction of that energy, but it sure has immeasurable potential in your hands.
  
It seems to have strange connection to your own cursed being, which is why you were

**The Essence**

![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/evil_essence.png) [Nefarious Essence](../../items/materials/evil-essence.md)

able to obtain this essence in the first place. It draws closer to you as you approach, as if bent by desire to latch onto you. Perhaps, you could allow it to do so?... No, what a dumb idea that would be.
  
But one thing is beyond questioning - this matter contains tremendous amounts

of dark, twisted power, that you just so happen to be capable of harnessing. You will surely find something to do with it.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/pure_heart.png) Pure Heart

With different materials, you may be able to try a production different from Twisted Heart. The combination of hidden dark pollution and faint divinity gave birth to this eerie creation with bright appearance.
  
Unlike another type of item, the Pure Heart and its creations typically provide protection and assistance,

rather than aggressive power.
  
But although it is a divine creation, it needs to rely on the power of curses, which is too ironic. So when using these items, please also pay attention to the hidden dangers.

*Recipe:* `enigmaticlegacyplus:pure_heart`

## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/sacred_crystal.png) Sacred Crystal

Tear apart those seemingly sacred creations and seize the cores of those Pure Ichor Spirits. The crystal is filled with a power unlike any other, pure and sacred, but the curse within your body does not seem to repel it.
  
The Pure Ichor Spirit's bizarre resistance and recovery effects have been perfectly inherited in this item, which

**The Crystal**

![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/sacred_crystal.png) [Sacred Crystal](../../items/generic/sacred-crystal.md)

can be used to craft some defense-themed items.
  
However, what is intriguing is the relationship between the curse and the power inherent in it. Can it be resisted? Can it be reversed? Can it be replaced?...

## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/abyssal_heart.png) Heart of the Abyss

When this unearthly abomination fell into your hands for the first time, description-defying mix of feelings have overcome your mind, among which there was a place for terror and excitement, admiration and disgust. The gaze of its lonely eye befell you with intent impossible to comprehend, but once initial wave of emotions retreated - you have

**The Heart**

![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/abyssal_heart.png) [Heart of the Abyss](../../items/materials/abyssal-heart.md)

concluded that it poses no threat to you.
  
Still, you know not what it truly is, and where it came from. Perhaps your insufferable existence, and efforts to live in spite of Seven Curses were noticed by some unnameable eldritch power, and through your

damnable being it have chosen to channel itself into the world. Perhaps it is a mere accident, a fluke in reality itself that allowed something like this to appear. Perhaps it is your ailing mind that have conceived this horror in some nightmarish fever, and with its growth - corruption of the curses have managed to manifest it into the mortal realm?
  
Perhaps it's best to never know, for some truths are much

more unsettling than anything a man's imagination can come up with.
  
Regardless, now you are the rightful owner of this thing. And it will not be long before you can put it to some unimaginably violent, frightening use... The world will suffer tenfold for all the suffering it caused to you.
