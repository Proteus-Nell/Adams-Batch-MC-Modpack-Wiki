## ![](../../../assets/icons/enigmaticlegacyplus/item/soul_crystal.png) Loss of Soul

Every death comes with its own tolls, but there is one far more significant than all others you had to face before.

With every fragment of your soul lost, you vitality fades, you become weaker and easier to kill. But should you approach your Soul Crystal, and it will lean towards you, and lost fragment of your soul will be instantly freed from its crystal prison, fusing together with your being again.

Hopefully, even under the worst circumstances, you won't lose all your life force. As fabric of your soul thins out, it becomes more and more resistant to being torn apart one more time.
  
It is safe to assume that at some point you will not be able to lose any more of your soul fragments, no matter how many times you die - though you would be left in an incredibly vulnerable state to any physical threat by then.
