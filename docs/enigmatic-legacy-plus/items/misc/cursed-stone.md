## Description

If you die in the Nether, in the middle of

sufficiently large lava lake with this stone

in your inventory, Ring of the Seven Curses

will be permanently destroyed, along with

one of your Soul Crystals.

This cannot be undone.

## What it does

Used to make [Holy Stone](bless-stone.md). It is made at the Crafting (cursed).
