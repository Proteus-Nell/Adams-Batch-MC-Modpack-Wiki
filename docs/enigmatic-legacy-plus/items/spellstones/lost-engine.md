## Description

Active ability:

- Absent.

Сooldown: ? seconds

Passive abilities:

- Enhance mobility and toughness.

- Reduce item cooldowns by 25%.

- Immune to Fall &amp; Explosion damage.

- Add 50% Crit damage.

- Golems will not target you actively.

- You are vulnerable to Magic Damage.

- The Lightning Damage received will become fatal.

Hold Shift to see details.

## What it does

Used to make [Non-Euclidean Cube](the-cube.md). It is made at the Spellstone Table and found in 1 kind of loot chest.
