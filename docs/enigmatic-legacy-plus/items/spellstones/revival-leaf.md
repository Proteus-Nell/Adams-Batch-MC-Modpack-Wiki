## Description

Active ability:

- surrounding mobs regeneration, at cost of experience.

Сooldown: ? seconds

Passive abilities:

- Slow natural regeneration.

- The attack target will be poisoned and reducing healing.

- Immune to Hunger, Poison and Withering effect.

- The surrounding crops are growing faster.

- All effects last longer.

- You can fly when there are plant blocks around.

- You are vulnerable to Fire Damage and Projectile Damage.

Hold Shift to see details.
