## Description

Active ability:

- Freeze the surrounding Mobs and deal massive damage.

Сooldown: ? seconds

Passive abilities:

- Freeze immunity and Frost Walker.

- Freeze those those who attack you in melee.

- Deal more damage to frozen targets.

- Once frozen to a certain degree, the target becomes difficult to move.

- 30% Projectile &amp; Sonic Damage Resistance.

- You are vulnerable to Fire Damage.

Hold Shift to see details.

## What it does

Used to make [Non-Euclidean Cube](the-cube.md). It is made at the Spellstone Table and found in 1 kind of loot chest.
