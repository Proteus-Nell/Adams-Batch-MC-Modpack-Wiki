## Description

Active ability:

- Teleports you to random location in one of three main dimensions.

Сooldown: ? seconds

Passive abilities:

- +35% Sprinting Speed, +100% Swimming Speed

- +60% Mining Speed, +40% Attack Speed

- +1 Fortune Level, +1 Luck

- Immunity to negative status effects.

- Any attacks that deal above ? damage are completely negated.

- 35% Chance to deflect an incoming attack or projectile.

- When attacked in melee, applies random debuff to attacker.

- Immunity to pressure, fall, collision, pricking, teleportation, fire

and lava damage.

- When you have more than 1 health, you survive any lethal damage

with 1 health remaining. If you have 1 health or less, attempts to

trigger active ability to prevent lethal damage. Restores your

health to 30% and applies powerful buffs if successful.

Hold Shift to see details.
