## Description

Active ability:

- Accelerates you in the direction of your sight.

Сooldown: ? seconds

Passive abilities:

- Immunity to fall and collision damage.

- 40% Chance to deflect incoming projectile.

- Your own projectiles are greatly accelerated.

- Active ability can be triggered by pressing jump

key while in mid-air.

- You are vulnerable to Withering and Void Damage.

Hold Shift to see details.
