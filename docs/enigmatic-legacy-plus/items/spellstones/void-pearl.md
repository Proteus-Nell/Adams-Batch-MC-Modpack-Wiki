## Description

Active ability:

- Absent.

Сooldown: ? seconds

Passive abilities:

- You no longer need air to breathe.

- Immunity to any status effects.

- Your attacks inflict Withering.

- Any creatures nearby you, if they are exposed to

darkness, receive massive damage and heavy debuffs.

Skyflaps are affected regardless of light level.

- 30% Chance that otherwise lethal damage won't kill you.

Hold Shift to see details.

## What it does

Used to make [The Arrogance of Chaos](../tools/chaos-elytra.md), [The Burden of Desolation](../rings/desolation-ring.md) and [Non-Euclidean Cube](the-cube.md). It is made at the Spellstone Table and found in 1 kind of loot chest.
