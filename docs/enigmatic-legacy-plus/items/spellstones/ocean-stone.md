## Description

Active ability:

- Starts thunderstorm, at great cost of experience.

Сooldown: ? seconds

Passive abilities:

- 40% Damage Resistance against underwater creatures.

- You no longer need air to breathe underwater.

- Provides Night Vision effect when in water.

- Provides effects of Aqua Affinity enchantment.

- Negates gravity when underwater.

- Greatly increased swimming speed.

- You are vulnerable to Fire Damage.

Hold Shift to see details.

## What it does

Used to make [Scroll of Thunder Embrace](../scrolls/thunder-scroll.md) and [Non-Euclidean Cube](the-cube.md). It is made at the Spellstone Table and found in 1 kind of loot chest.
