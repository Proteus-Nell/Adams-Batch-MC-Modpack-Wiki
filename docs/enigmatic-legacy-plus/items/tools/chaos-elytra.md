## Description

Hold jump key while flying to continuously

accelerate forward. Elytra is damaged

faster when accelerating.

Reduce ? of damage from back.

When accelerating vertically towards

the ground, it will cause range damage.

Abyss Boost:

- Improves all aspects of this item.
