## Description

When in the inventory or curio slot:

Increase damage to Illagers by 80%.

Reduce damage from Illagers by 50%.

Can avoid death when held or equipped.

Causing damage to the surroundings when triggered.

## What it does

It is made at the Crafting (cursed).
