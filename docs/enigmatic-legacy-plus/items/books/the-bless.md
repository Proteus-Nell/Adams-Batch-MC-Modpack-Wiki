## Description

Alteration of the ?:

Fourth Curse

- Always deals its full damage.

When carried in inventory:

- Extinguishes you when on fire.

- Extended invincibility duration after taking damage.

Remove the beneficial effects on the target and

probably apply Ichor Corrosion effect.

Causing more damage to the target on fire .

In the endless journey of suffering,

using this to light up the way forward.

- Reduce the effect of the Fourth Curse by 20%.

The blinding light of revelation shall

incinerate those who refuse to embrace it.
