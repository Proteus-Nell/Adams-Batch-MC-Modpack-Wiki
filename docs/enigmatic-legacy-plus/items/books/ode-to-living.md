## Description

When carried in inventory:

- Your attacks can't deal damage to animals.

- Hobgoblins around you are rendered passive.

- All damage your pets receive within ? blocks

from you is redirected upon you.

- Redirected damage is reduced by 50%.

Alteration of the ?:

Second Curse

- Neutral creatures stay neutral towards you.

Attack with this item to prevent this kind of animal

from being protected by this item.

Use to appease animals and restore protective effects.

## What it does

It is crafted.
