## Description

Whenever you die while having it equipped, summons

Extradimensional Vessel that safely stores your items,

Soul Crystals and Experience.

Approach the vessel to regain what was lost.

Attack Strength provides a damage reduction effect.

You take no damage from unattacked targets.

When equipped as Amulet:

+1 Charm Slot

+2 Luck

+4 Damage Reduction

+8 Armor Toughness

## What it does

It is made at the Crafting (cursed).
