## Description

Whenever you die while having it equipped, summons

Extradimensional Vessel that safely stores your items,

Soul Crystals and Experience.

Approach the vessel to regain what was lost.

It appears oddly reminiscent of something, yet

you still remain unable to recall what exactly.

Time slowly washes away last traces of those

lost memories...

Name is engraved upon it: ?

## What it does

Used to make [The Testament of Contempt](eldritch-amulet.md). It is crafted.
