## Description

When worn, your inventory persists with you

after death. Experience and Soul Crystals will

still be lost.

Creatures beneath your gaze will be slowed

down and weakened.

Abyss Boost:

- Deal 80% more damage to creatures with less health than you.

Name is engraved upon it: ?

When equipped as Amulet:

+1 Charm Slot

+20% Attack Damage

+15% Lifesteal
