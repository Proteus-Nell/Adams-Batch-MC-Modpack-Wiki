## Description

To contempt the gods, one must know them...

Consume to obtain +1 Charm Slot permanently.

## What it does

Edible, but it doesn't fill you up. Eating it gives [Absorption](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Absorption) V for 60 s, [Regeneration](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Regeneration) III for 30 s, [Fire Resistance](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fire_Resistance) for 160 s and [Pure Resistance](../../effects/pure-resistance.md) III for 60 s.
