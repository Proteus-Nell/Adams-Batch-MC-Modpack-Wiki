## Description

A container similar to a Ender Chest.

The books stored in will be considered

as being in the inventory.

The item still works in the Ender Chest.

## What it does

It is found in 2 kinds of loot chest.

## Obtaining

### Loot

| Source | Count | Chance | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chest: End Simple Addon (`enigmaticlegacyplus:chests/end_simple_addon`) | 1 | 3.0% |  |
