## Description

It is connected to location beyond your space and

time. You can awaken the eye, if you wish to risk

drawing attention of someone... or something.

Now that you have drawn his attention, whoever uses

the eye to observe you and the world does not seem to

need it in order to reach you. Whether he is incapable

or simply unwilling to establish any contact beyond

mental remains a mystery.
