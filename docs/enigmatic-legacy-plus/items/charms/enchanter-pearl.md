## Description

+1 Charm Slot

You receive greater amount and power of

enchantments when using Enchanting Table.

Enchanting has chance to produce both

curses and treasure enchantments.

You do not require Lapis Lazuli to enchant.

## What it does

Used to make [The Infinitum](../books/the-infinitum.md). It is made at the Crafting (cursed).
