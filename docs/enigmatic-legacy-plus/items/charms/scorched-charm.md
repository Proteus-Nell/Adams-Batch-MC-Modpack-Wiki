## Description

Immunize the wearer against the fire damage

And be able to walk on the lava.

Obtain natural regeneration when in contact with lava.

Trigger lifesteal when attacking a target on fire.

? chance to resist the attack damage.

## What it does

It is made at the Crafting (cursed).
