## Description

+1.0% Attack Damage

+1.0% Attack Speed

+0.5% Movement Speed

+0.5% Damage Resistance

These traits are multiplied by how many percents

of your health pool you're currently missing.

When any damage received takes Blazing Might away,

the damage will be reduced according to the amplifier.

Current traits:

+? Attack Damage

+? Attack Speed

+? Movement Speed

+? Damage Resistance
