## Description

Provides you an ability to freely fly, at the

cost of quickly consuming your experience.

Experience consumption is completely negated

within the range of an active Beacon.

Compensates mining speed penalty when flying.

Provides you an ability to fly within the range

of an active Beacon, at the cost of slowly

consuming your experience.
