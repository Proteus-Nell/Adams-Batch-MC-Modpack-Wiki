## Description

You deal +100% Damage to players who are not

chosen, and your melee attacks set them ablaze.

When you receive lethal damage, your health is

restored to 50% and powerful buffs are applied.

This ability has 600 seconds cooldown.

Remaining Cooldown: 0s

## What it does

It is crafted.
