## Description

Deactivated

Absorption

Extraction

Can store limitless amount of experience.

The scroll interaction is same as Ageless Wisdom.

Improve your abilities based on experience level,

But the EXP gained from experience orb will decrease,

Lose all experiences after death.

While active, collects experience orbs

within ? blocks radius.

Mode: ?

Stored Experience:

? units, or ? levels

Current Keybind: ?

Arcane Scroll Active Ability

Press Shift+Right-Click while holding it

to toggle it active.

Press Right-Click to switch modes.

You can also use related keybind.

In Absorption mode, it will consume your

experience, while carried in scroll slot.

In Extraction mode, this behavior is

reversed, and the scroll transfers all

stored experience back to you.
