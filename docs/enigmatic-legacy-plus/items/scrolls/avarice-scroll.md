## Description

+1 Fortune Level

Renders Piglins neutral towards you, despite

the Second Curse. Bartering with them is

around 100% more beneficial.

Provides 15% Chance of 1 Emerald dropping

from any slain mob.

Villager trades have 35% Discount.
