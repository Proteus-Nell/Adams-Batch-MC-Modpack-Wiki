## Description

Deactivated

Press Shift+Right-Click while holding it

to toggle it active.

Press Right-Click to switch modes.

You can also use related keybind.

In Absorption mode, it will consume your

experience, while carried in scroll slot.

In Extraction mode, this behavior is

reversed, and the scroll transfers all

stored experience back to you.
