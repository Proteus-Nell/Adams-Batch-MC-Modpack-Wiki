## Description

After consuming the fruit you shall never

experience hunger again, but any form of

regeneration will have 80% weaker effect.

Knowledge is the greatest gift.

## What it does

Food. Used to make [The Forbidden Juice](../potions/forbidden-juice.md). It is found in 1 kind of loot chest.
