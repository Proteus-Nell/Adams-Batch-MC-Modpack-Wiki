## Description

Seven curses will befall whoever bears it:

- You receive double damage from ANY source.

- Neutral creatures are aggressive towards you.

- Armor is 30% less effective.

- Monsters receive 40% less damage from you.

- When on fire, you burn forever.

- Every death tears your soul apart.

- You suffer from incurable insomnia.

Seven blessings will reward those who withstand:

- +1 Looting Level

- +1 Fortune Level

- +300% Experience dropped.

- +10 Enchanting Power in Enchanting Table.

- Unique drops from some creatures.

- Functionality of Ring of Ender.

- You can create and use unique relics.

Once forged by antediluvian god, it beckons

mortals and higher beings alike with promise

of untold riches and immeasurable might...

Be it arrogance or ignorance that leads them

to believe they can harness ring's power,

both are paid for in suffering the extent of

which defies description.

Once worn, it becomes a part of you.

With the power of god, you can unequip it.

This ring will persist with you forever.
