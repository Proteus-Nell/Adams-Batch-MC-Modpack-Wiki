## Description

Consumes fuel bar to smelt items as blast furnace.

Fuel bar can be naturally restored or increased by

right-clicking with fuel on the ring.

Automatically smelt dropped items during mining.

Hold Shift to smelt nearby dropped items.

## What it does

It is crafted.
