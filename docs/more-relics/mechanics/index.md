# Mechanics

More Relics is an addon for [Relics](../../relics/mechanics/index.md). Its relics work the same way: find them in chests, use them to earn experience, and spend levels on their abilities. See [Items](../items/index.md) for every relic with its abilities, starting stats and max-level values.

The [Configs](../configs/index.md) page covers the mod's general settings. Individual relic stats can be changed through Relics' extended configs, which the mod generates under `config/morerelics/relics` once they are enabled.
