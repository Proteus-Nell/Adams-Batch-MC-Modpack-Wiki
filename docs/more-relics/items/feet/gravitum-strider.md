## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **8**, first level costs **120** XP, +40 XP per level.

> A boot that uses a Gravitum engine to reduce the effects of gravity. It was commonly used by astronauts for training before the severe health risks of Gravitum were discovered.

Found in loot chests of kind: End and stronghold chests, any End chest, chests in the deep dark.

### Elevated Steps

*Passive. Ability max level 5.*

Reduces your gravity by **30-40 (up to 50-60)**%. Fall damage and safe fall distance are adjusted accordingly.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gravity Reduction | 30-40 | +4 per level | 50-60 |

### Leap

*Unlocks at relic level 5, active (toggleable). Ability max level 3.*

Jumping after crouching for one second launches you upwards with a force of **0.2-0.28 (up to 0.32-0.4)**. You take reduced fall damage from this jump.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jump Force | 0.2-0.28 | +0.04 per level | 0.32-0.4 |
