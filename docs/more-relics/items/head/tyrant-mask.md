## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **1**, first level costs **100** XP, +30 XP per level.

> A brass mask once worn by a forgotten tyrant. Though his soul is long gone the mask still brims with malice, drawing restless spirits and trapping them within its dark influence.

Found in loot chests of kind: bastion chests.

### Regrets

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

The trapped souls within the mask hinder you, lowering your speed, damage, and attack speed by **20**%.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Debuff | 20 | - | - |

### Revenge

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

The souls trapped within the mask crave for revenge against the monsters that slayed them. Perhaps hunting monsters with them could soothe their fury.

### Monotony

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

The souls trapped within the mask regret avoiding change and living a monotonous life. Perhaps travelling with them could soothe their longing for adventure.

### Futility

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

The souls trapped within the mask carry the sorrow of a life spent without leaving a lasting mark. Maybe building with them will help soothe their need to create.
