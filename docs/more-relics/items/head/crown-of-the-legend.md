## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **8**, first level costs **100** XP, +40 XP per level.

> This crown embodies courage. Those who wear it inherit the legacy of a true legend, unshaken in the face of any challenge.

Found in loot chests of kind: bastion chests, any Nether chest.

### King Pig

*Passive. Ability max level 3.*

Increases your damage against pig based mobs by **0.2-0.4 (up to 0.5-0.7)**X.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perch | 0.2-0.4 | +0.1 per level | 0.5-0.7 |

### Not Even Close

*Unlocks at relic level 3, passive. Ability max level 5.*

Grants life-steal scaling with damage and missing health.  
(1-(Current Health)/(Maximum Health)) * Damage * **0.1-0.2 (up to 0.2-0.3)**

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Potency | 0.1-0.2 | +0.02 per level | 0.2-0.3 |
