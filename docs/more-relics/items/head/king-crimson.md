## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **14**, first level costs **10** XP, +30 XP per level.

> Freed souls burst out of the mask, changing its color and leaving a strange hole in the middle. When you look into the hole you can't see the bottom but the other side looks solid.

### Epitaph

*Active (instantaneous). Ability max level 4.*

Distorts time and space, rewinding you to your position from 10 seconds ago. **200-140 (up to 80-20)** seconds cooldown.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooldown | 200-140 | -30 per level | 80-20 |

### Talk to The Wind

*Unlocks at relic level 3, passive. Ability max level 5.*

Your attacks disrupt your target's mind, applying Weakness and Slowness with a **2-4 (up to 7-9)**% chance for **3-6 (up to 7-10)** seconds. If the target is another player Static is also applied for half the duration.  
Your first attack after Epitaph always triggers this effect.  
  
Static makes players unable to see other mobs and players while it is active.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seconds | 3-6 | +0.8 per level | 7-10 |
| Chance | 2-4 | +1 per level | 7-9 |

### The Court Of The Crimson King

*Unlocks at relic level 7, passive. Ability max level 5.*

When you attack, if your maximum health is more than or equal to your target's maximum health and their health is below **9-14 (up to 17-22)**%, they are instantly executed. If your target has more maximum health than you they instead take double damage below this threshold.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Threshold | 9-14 | +1.6 per level | 17-22 |
