## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **5**, first level costs **120** XP, +20 XP per level.

> A silver necklace adorned with a small opal stone, traditionally gifted to travelers and soldiers venturing into barren lands, symbolizing a wish for their safety and protection.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in desert biomes, chests in snowy biomes.
