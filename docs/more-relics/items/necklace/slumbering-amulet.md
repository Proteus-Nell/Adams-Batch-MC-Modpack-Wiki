## Description

Dormant Potential

## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **75** XP, +50 XP per level.

> A simple, unremarkable necklace lacking distinction, yet something about it feels off as if it were destined for a greater purpose.

### Odd Aura

*Passive. Ability max level 1.*

This relic brings a soothing comfort, making you feel compelled to wear it more.

### Awakening Potential

*Unlocks at relic level 10, passive. Ability max level 1.*

While wearing the amulet, thoughts of Nether Stars fill your mind, and visions of them appear in your dreams. A strong urge to hold one begins to rise within you.
