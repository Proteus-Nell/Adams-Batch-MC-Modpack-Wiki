## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **5**, first level costs **240** XP, +60 XP per level.

> A wrist attachment with dual curved blades pointing outward. It is designed to be used alongside a primary weapon, striking the moment the foe's attention is diverted. This unusual style is a favorite among bandits and assassins as it can make even the most seasoned opponents slip up.

### Two Fold

*Passive. Ability max level 5.*

Divides your attacks into two parts dealing **40-42.5 (up to 47.5-50)**% and **60-62.5 (up to 67.5-70)**% of your damage each.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smaller Strike Per | 40-42.5 | +1.5 per level | 47.5-50 |
| Bigger Strike Per | 60-62.5 | +1.5 per level | 67.5-70 |

### Blindside

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 10.*

Makes it so your attacks cannot be blocked with shields.
