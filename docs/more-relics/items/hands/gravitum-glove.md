## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **9**, first level costs **90** XP, +20 XP per level.

> A glove which is made from a unique cloth-like material which anchors itself to its current position, making it very hard to move. When it collides with an object it transfers a massive amount of energy, creating a powerful impact even at low speed.

Found in loot chests of kind: End and stronghold chests, any End chest, chests in the deep dark.

### All In

*Passive. Ability max level 5.*

Multiplies attack damage by **1.4-1.7 (up to 1.7-2)**X but reduces attack speed by 66%.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage Boost | 1.4-1.7 | +0.06 per level | 1.7-2 |

### Bonk

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 4.*

Attacks over 20 damage (before armor) have a **3-5 (up to 8-10)**% chance to trigger a heavy strike, dealing 2X damage and inflicting Dazed for **4-6 (up to 14-16)** seconds.  
Dazed enemies have a chance to miss their attacks.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chance | 3-5 | +1.25 per level | 8-10 |
| Duration | 4-6 | +2.5 per level | 14-16 |
