## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **5**, first level costs **20** XP, +20 XP per level.

> A symbiotic worm that manipulates its host's emotions, helping them adapt and thrive in shifting conditions.

Found in loot chests of kind: any chest.

### Moody

*Passive. Ability max level 5.*

The worm changes its mood every 10 minutes, providing different benefits:  
Furious: Increases damage by **0.1-0.15 (up to 0.2-0.25)**X and attacks burn enemies.  
Joyful: Increases speed and luck by **0.1-0.15 (up to 0.2-0.25)**X.  
Gloomy: Reduces damage taken by **10-15 (up to 20-25)**% and increases resistance to knockback.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multip | 0.1-0.15 | +0.02 per level | 0.2-0.25 |
| Reduction | 10-15 | +2 per level | 20-25 |

### Tranquillity

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 10.*

The worm can switch to a new mood.  
Tranquil: Provides all the advantages of the worm's other moods.
