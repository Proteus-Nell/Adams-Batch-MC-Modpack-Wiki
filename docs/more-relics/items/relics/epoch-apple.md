## What it does

Food.

## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **12**, first level costs **100** XP, +40 XP per level.

> An ancient apple believed to be from the earliest generations of its kind. Its regenerative abilities allow it to recover from severe damage which is how it has survived through the ages.

### Persistent

*Passive. Ability max level 8.*

The apple recovers to its full form after **16-12 (up to 12-8)** minutes of being eaten.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooldown | 16-12 | -0.5 per level | 12-8 |

### Ripen

*Unlocks at relic level 8, passive. Ability max level 4.*

The apple ripens into greater quality after **60-52 (up to 48-40)** minutes of being in its full form.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooldown | 60-52 | -3 per level | 48-40 |
