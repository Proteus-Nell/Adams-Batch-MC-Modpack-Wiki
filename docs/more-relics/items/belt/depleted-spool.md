## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **1**, first level costs **100** XP, +20 XP per level.

> Once able to regenerate threads that could alter reality, this spool has lost its power. Now silent and empty, it stands as a relic of forgotten potential.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in cave biomes, mineshaft chests, chests in mountain biomes.

### Restoration

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

The spool's powers can be restored by holding the required items while wearing it, reawakening its ability to regenerate reality-altering threads.
