## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **12**, first level costs **140** XP, +40 XP per level.

> Restored to its former power, the spool now regenerates silk that can subtly alter reality, weaving outcomes according to the weaver’s design.

### Weaver

*Passive. Ability max level 3.*

Each strike against an enemy weaves silk back into your spool, building up to a maximum of 10 silk stored.  
Additionally, gain silk passively every **7-5 (up to 5.5-3.5)** seconds when you have less than 3 silk.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passive Regen Time | 7-5 | -0.5 per level | 5.5-3.5 |

### Bind

*Active (instantaneous). Ability max level 3.*

Consumes 6 silk to heal for **2-4 (up to 3.5-5.5)** and grants absorption for a short duration, shielding you from further harm.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Healing Amount | 2-4 | +0.5 per level | 3.5-5.5 |

### Soar

*Unlocks at relic level 3, active (instantaneous). Ability max level 3.*

Consumes 3 silk to propel you in the direction you're facing with a force of **1.1-1.3 (up to 1.4-1.6)** and grants a speed boost for **7-10 (up to 13-16)** seconds afterwards.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leap Power | 1.1-1.3 | +0.1 per level | 1.4-1.6 |
| Speed Duration | 7-10 | +2 per level | 13-16 |

### Unravel

*Unlocks at relic level 6, active (instantaneous). Ability max level 3.*

Consumes 7 silk to unleash a blast that deals **4-6 (up to 10-12)** damage and severely slows enemies for **2-3 (up to 3.5-4.5)** seconds within a 4-block radius around you.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage | 4-6 | +2 per level | 10-12 |
| Slow Duration | 2-3 | +0.5 per level | 3.5-4.5 |
