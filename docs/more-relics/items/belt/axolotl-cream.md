## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **3**, first level costs **120** XP, +20 XP per level.

> A light pink cream. Enhances healing and smells like strawberries.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in ocean/river biomes, chests in jungle/tropical biomes.
