## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **5**, first level costs **100** XP, +20 XP per level.

> A flawless orb with stark white corners, its center a deep purple that seems to pulse with a quiet, simmering anger. It radiates an unsettling aura, as if it harbors a grudge.

Found in loot chests of kind: bastion chests, chests in desert biomes, fire/Nether-themed chests (and ruined portals).

### Guts

*Passive. Ability max level 5.*

If you are under a negative effect or on fire, deal **0.2-0.4 (up to 0.45-0.65)**X extra damage.
