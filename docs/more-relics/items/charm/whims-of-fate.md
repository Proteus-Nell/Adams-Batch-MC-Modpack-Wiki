## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **8**, first level costs **100** XP, +25 XP per level.

> A cursed die that draws strength from its wielder's despair and suffering, promising salvation while demanding agony in return.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in desert biomes, bastion chests.

### Just One More Roll

*Passive. Ability max level 8.*

Whenever the user is hit, rolls a 20-sided die. Each number above 1 increases damage reduction by **0.5-1 (up to 1.3-1.8)**%. A 20 cancels all damage, a 1 doubles it.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage Reduction Per | 0.5-1 | +0.1 per level | 1.3-1.8 |
