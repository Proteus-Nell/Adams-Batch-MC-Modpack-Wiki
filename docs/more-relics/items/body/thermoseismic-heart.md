## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +25 XP per level.

> A high-tech heart implant that converts movement and vibrations into energy, powering itself and recharging its internal batteries.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in desert biomes.

### Pyromania

*Passive. Ability max level 3.*

Explosions do not harm the user and instead grant a stack of Overload for **20-30 (up to 35-45)** seconds, with a maximum of 4 stacks.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duration | 20-30 | +5 per level | 35-45 |

### Thermal Discharge

*Unlocks at relic level 3, active (instantaneous). Ability max level 4.*

When activated, it releases a stream of heat in a **1-2 (up to 2-3)** block radius, dealing **3-5 (up to 8-10)** damage. Stacks of Overload are consumed to amplify the fire's power and range.  
While cyberpsychosis is active: The fire triggers as if the user has 2 Overload stacks when they have less and the Overload stacks are not consumed.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Radius | 1-2 | +0.25 per level | 2-3 |
| Damage | 3-5 | +1.25 per level | 8-10 |

### Cyberpsychosis

*Unlocks at relic level 7, passive. Ability max level 3.*

Killing an enemy has a **3-5 (up to 6-8)**% chance to trigger cyberpsychosis for **4-7 (up to 10-13)** seconds.  
The chance and duration stacks with other Cyberware with this ability.  
Cyberpsychosis: Significantly improves Cyberware abilities but erodes sanity, making the familiar foreign.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chance | 3-5 | +1 per level | 6-8 |
| Duration | 4-7 | +2 per level | 10-13 |
