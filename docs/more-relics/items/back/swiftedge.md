## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **8**, first level costs **125** XP, +25 XP per level.

> A magic-infused blade forged by Magna of the legendary twin smiths. It is said to be sharp enough to split the wind itself.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in mountain biomes.

### Head Start

*Passive. Ability max level 4.*

Upon entering combat, the damage of your first **2-3 (up to 4-5)** attacks is increased by **0.2-0.3 (up to 0.4-0.5)**X.  
  
You are considered out of combat if you haven't dealt or taken damage for 15 seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage Perch | 0.2-0.3 | +0.05 per level | 0.4-0.5 |
| Hit Count | 2-3 | +0.5 per level | 4-5 |

### Modal Soul

*Unlocks at relic level 4, passive. Ability max level 4.*

You gain a **0.12-0.24 (up to 0.28-0.4)**X speed boost while out of combat.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed Perch | 0.12-0.24 | +0.04 per level | 0.28-0.4 |

### Blood Bond

*Unlocks at relic level 8, passive. Ability max level 10.*

If you also have Runic Plate equipped, Head Start's boosted attack count is increased by 50%.
