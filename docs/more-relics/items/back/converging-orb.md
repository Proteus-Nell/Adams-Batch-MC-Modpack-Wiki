## Description

Density

Task: Get hit the Warden's sonic boom.

The orb exceeded critical density, collapsing in on itself to form a miniature black hole.

## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **1**, first level costs **1** XP, +1 XP per level.

> A rather heavy orb made from an unknown material which permanently compresses under pressure.

### Convergence

*Passive. Ability max level 1.*

Every time the user is hit the orb undergoes permanent compression, decreasing its size and dramatically increasing its density.
