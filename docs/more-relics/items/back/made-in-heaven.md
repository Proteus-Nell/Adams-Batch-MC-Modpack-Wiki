## Description

Infinite Equilibrium

Zenith has shielded you from death and is now on cooldown.

Zenith is ready to revive again.

## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **18**, first level costs **200** XP, +70 XP per level.

> The amulet has unlocked its full potential, transforming into its true form. It now seems to share a bond with you.

### As One

*Passive. Ability max level 8.*

Made in Heaven channels its power into you, increasing all your stats by **0.03-0.06 (up to 0.07-0.1)**X.  
Hitting an enemy grants Accelerating, stacking up to 10 times. Each stack boosts all stats except health by 0.03X. Gaining stacks has a **44-32 (up to 28-16)** second cooldown and all stacks expire after a short duration of not attacking.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initial Boost | 0.03-0.06 | +0 per level | 0.07-0.1 |
| Accel Cooldown | 44-32 | -2 per level | 28-16 |

### Zenith

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 3.*

While at 10 Accelerating stacks gain an additional **0.08-0.14 (up to 0.14-0.2)**X stat boost and automatically revive with half your maximum HP when you die.  
Triggering the revive puts it on cooldown for **1200-860 (up to 1080-740)** seconds and consumes all Accelerating stacks.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revive Cooldown | 1200-860 | -40 per level | 1080-740 |
| Stat Boost | 0.08-0.14 | +0.02 per level | 0.14-0.2 |

### Condemn

*Unlocks at relic level 8, active (instantaneous). Ability max level 4.*

Upon activation, unleash holy fire in a cone, dealing **4-6 (up to 6-8)** damage, burning enemies and applying Charred for a duration.  
Charred causes affected enemies to take 1 extra damage per level from all sources.  
Cooldown: **60-45 (up to 40-25)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooldown | 60-45 | -5 per level | 40-25 |
| Damage | 4-6 | +0.5 per level | 6-8 |

### Divine Judgement

*Unlocks at relic level 12, active (interruptible). Ability max level 3.*

Upon activation, rise into the air and after a short delay engulf everything within a 7-block radius in holy fire, dealing up to **40-55 (up to 70-85)** damage over 5 seconds in 5 increasing hits and burning enemies. The last 3 hits apply and stack Charred.  
You gain 75% damage reduction while casting this ability.  
This ability can only be used while you have 10 Accelerating stacks and has a cooldown of **1260-960 (up to 1080-780)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage | 40-55 | +10 per level | 70-85 |
| Cooldown | 1260-960 | -60 per level | 1080-780 |
