## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **13**, first level costs **300** XP, +60 XP per level.

> The orb has collapsed in on itself, becoming a miniature black hole. It now absorbs any harm aimed at its user, redirecting it toward those who oppose them.

### Entropy

*Passive. Ability max level 5.*

**7-10 (up to 17-20)**% of the incoming damage is also dealt to the attacker.  
  
Reflected damage cannot exceed the user's current health.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perch | 7-10 | +2 per level | 17-20 |

### Flow of Calamity

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 5.*

When attacked, the attacker suffers a random negative potion effect.  
Cooldown: **16-12 (up to 11-7)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooldown | 16-12 | -1 per level | 11-7 |

### Event Horizon

*Unlocks at relic level 10, active (instantaneous). Ability max level 3.*

Upon activation, the user unleashes a blast that knocks back and stuns nearby enemies.  
For the next **6-10 (up to 12-16)** seconds, the user enters a state of nullification, gaining complete invincibility and doubling the effects of Entropy and Flow of Calamity. This ability has a cooldown of **540-420 (up to 450-330)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duration | 6-10 | +2 per level | 12-16 |
| Cooldown | 540-420 | -30 per level | 450-330 |
