## What it does

Elite Tensura's **Forge**: right-click the station to pick a recipe and forge one-of-a-kind gear through a minigame (Hammer Timing, Rune Alignment, Magicule Flow, and Dragonfire Forging for the toughest recipes). The better you play, the better the **quality** of the result, which multiplies its stats:

| Quality | Stats | Durability |
|---|---|---|
| Poor | ×0.85 | ×0.9 |
| Common | ×1 | ×1 |
| Fine | ×1.1 | ×1.15 |
| Superior | ×1.25 | ×1.35 |
| Masterwork | ×1.45 | ×1.6 |
| Legendary | ×1.7 | ×2 |
| Mythical | ×2 | ×2.5 |

Legendary and Mythical are only reached through lucky **critical upgrades** (5% base chance, more for a high minigame score). Forged gear can also carry **engravings**, and the station's Salvage button breaks forged items back down into part of their materials.

Make a Basic station from 8 iron ingots around an anvil. A station **upgrades itself** as you use it: Basic becomes Magisteel after 20 crafts, Magisteel becomes Mithril after 40 and Mithril becomes Dragonforge after 80. Higher tiers can forge everything the lower ones can. The Forge chapter of the Elite Tensura Codex has the full rules.
