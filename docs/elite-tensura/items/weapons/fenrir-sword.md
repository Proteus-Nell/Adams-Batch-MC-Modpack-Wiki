## What it does

A frost sword. Every hit gives the target [Fenrir Effect](../../effects/fenrir-sword-effect.md) V for 3 seconds. When you swing at a creature, every non-ally within 5 blocks gets Fenrir's frost V for 5 seconds and water within 2 blocks of the target freezes into ice. Repair it with Stellar Gold Coins.

A sword that deals **180** attack damage at **4** attack speed. It also has +0.8% critical hit chance, +2 critical damage multiplier and 50% sweeping damage. Durability: **5,000**.
