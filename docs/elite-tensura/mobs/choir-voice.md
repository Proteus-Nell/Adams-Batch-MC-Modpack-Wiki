## What it does

The **Hollow Choir**: a Heart (6,000 health) surrounded by orbiting **Voices**. The Heart re-splits into new Voices at 66% and 33% health (6, then 5, then 4 Voices).

- While at least 3 Voices live, the **Choir Ward** deflects most direct damage from the Heart (only 15% gets through). Damage you deal to a Voice also carries 35% over to the Heart.
- The Voices sing an aura within 14 blocks that grows stronger the longer you stay in their line of sight.
- The Heart releases a **Dirge** pulse around itself every 8 s.

It's one of the calamity bosses Elite Tensura can pick, but this pack's calamity list doesn't include it, so it only appears if spawned by hand.

A boss.
