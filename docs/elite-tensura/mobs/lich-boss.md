## What it does

The **Lich**, one of Elite Tensura's calamity bosses. Stats: 5,000 health, 280 armor, 80 attack damage. How it fights:

- It summons waves of 4 to 6 tethered minions (a new wave 60 s after the last one dies, from phase 2). While any minion is alive it takes only **10%** of the damage.
- It heals for 80% of the damage it deals, and regenerates by draining the area's magicule.
- No single hit can take more than 4% of its max health.
- Holy and fire damage hurt it **×1.25**.
- Once per fight, **Last Rites** lets it defy death.
- It only starts fights with players who have at least 10,000 max EP.

**Calamities:** every 2 to 6 hours (when at least 2 players with 100,000+ max EP are online), Elite Tensura spawns a calamity boss with ×3 health and ×2 damage, usually on a Walpurgis territory claim. It's protected by a **resonance shield** that several different players must all hit within 15 s to shatter, which leaves it vulnerable for 20 s. No hit can deal more than 50 damage, its healing is cut to 25%, and it leaves after 30 minutes if nobody beats it. Everyone who dealt at least 10% of its health gets a Rare crate key and the Calamity Slayer title, the top damage dealer an Elite key, and everyone who hurt it a Potential Catalyst.

A boss. Drops [Hihi'Irokane Ingot](../../tensura-reincarnated/items/materials/hihiirokane-ingot.md), [Chronicle](../items/miscellaneous/chronicle.md) and [Potential Catalyst](../items/miscellaneous/potential-catalyst.md).
