## What it does

The **Gravebound Colossus**, a calamity boss that never moves: it fights everything within 48 blocks. Stats: 8,000 health, 200 armor, 60 attack damage.

- Three **Sigils** (400 health each) protect it: while any stands, the Colossus takes only 20% of the damage and the rest hits a Sigil. Breaking all three leaves it **Exposed** for 12 s, taking ×2 damage.
- **Slam Shockwave** every 6 s (a ring up to 12 blocks), and **Grave Spire** columns every 8 s against ranged attackers.
- From phase 2: **Gravity Well** pulls everyone within 24 blocks, and **Sunder Zones** hurt anyone standing in them.
- Its attacks partly pierce resistances and barriers.
- If nobody fights it for 30 s, it regenerates 2% of its health per second.

**Calamities:** every 2 to 6 hours (when at least 2 players with 100,000+ max EP are online), Elite Tensura spawns a calamity boss with ×3 health and ×2 damage, usually on a Walpurgis territory claim. It's protected by a **resonance shield** that several different players must all hit within 15 s to shatter, which leaves it vulnerable for 20 s. No hit can deal more than 50 damage, its healing is cut to 25%, and it leaves after 30 minutes if nobody beats it. Everyone who dealt at least 10% of its health gets a Rare crate key and the Calamity Slayer title, the top damage dealer an Elite key, and everyone who hurt it a Potential Catalyst.

A boss.
