## What it does

A mark left by the [Void Edge](../items/weapons/void-edge.md). It does nothing by itself, but the Void Edge's teleport ability jumps you behind the nearest marked enemy within 20 blocks and strikes harder against it. The Astral Edge's big attack consumes the mark for **×1.5** damage. Lasts 6 s.
