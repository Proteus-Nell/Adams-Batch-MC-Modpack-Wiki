## What it does

Kaioken, a transformation with four tiers (the tier you can reach depends on your EP: 100,000, 1,000,000 and 10,000,000 for tiers II to IV).

- Max magicule and aura grow by **+1×** at tier I, +2× at II, +9× at III and +19× at IV (×2, ×3, ×10 and ×20 by default), and both are refilled when it starts.
- Per tier: **+8** attack damage, **+5** armor and **+0.02** movement speed.
- Every second it burns **1** health per tier (never below 10% of your max) and **50** magicule and aura per tier.

When it ends, your magicule and aura are divided back down, and you're exhausted: [Weakness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Weakness) II, [Fragility](../../tensura-reincarnated/effects/fragility.md) II and [Paralysis](../../tensura-reincarnated/effects/paralysis.md) I for 600 s. You can't use Kaioken again while you have Fragility.
