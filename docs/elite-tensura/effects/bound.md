## What it does

Bound in Hephaestus's chains. Per level: **-30%** attack damage, **-30%** armor and **-80%** movement speed. Hephaestus's Chains inflict level 1 + 1 for 6 s.

## Applied by

[Hephaestus, Lord of Creation](../abilities/ultimate-skills/hephaestus.md)
