## What it does

Ouroboros's rebirth is ready. While you have it, dying doesn't end things: when you respawn you're sent back to where you died with **30%** of your health, a few seconds of invulnerability, and [Cycle Debt](cycle-debt.md). It doesn't work against severance or soul-destroying damage. Arming it costs half your max magicule and lasts 300 s.
