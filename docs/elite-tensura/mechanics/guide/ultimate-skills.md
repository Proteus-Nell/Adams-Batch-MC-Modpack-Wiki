# Ultimate Skills

<small>[Elite Tensura](../../index.md) &rsaquo; [Elite Tensura Codex](index.md)</small>

The pinnacle of power. Ultimate Skills reshape battle and self alike.

## Basilisk Skin

Type: Ultimate Skill
  
A living suit of armor that hardens with your power. The plating is fixed at the moment you toggle it on, by your EP then:
- High Magisteel
- Pure Magisteel - 200,000 EP
- Adamantite - 800,000 EP

- Hihiirokane - 1,000,000 EP
  
Toggle it on to wear the armor - it costs nothing to keep up, but switches itself off the moment you put real armor on. While worn it shields you from magicule poison.

**How to Unlock**

Master **Crocodile Skin**, become a True Demon Lord or True Hero, and reach **10,000,000 EP**.
  
Learning Basilisk Skin consumes Crocodile Skin.
  
*Servers may relax these requirements via config.*

## Beelzebuth, Lord of Gluttony

Type: Ultimate Skill
  
The Lord of Gluttony's absorption economy. A six-mode skill built around devouring, storing, and redistributing power:
- Predation
- Stomach
- Isolate
- Food Chain: Provide
- Food Chain: Receive
- Soul Consume

Where Ouroboros wields Gluttony through raw combat, Beelzebuth channels it through consumption, storage, and conversion.

**Predation &amp; Stomach**

Hold to unleash a devouring mist that drains magicule, steals skills, and bites for a flat **300** a pulse - killed targets and their dropped items are swept straight into your **Stomach**, a spatial storage **108** slots deep.

**Isolate**

A one-way quarantine: sealed items and cleansed debuffs can't be retrieved, only converted. Sneak-press to convert everything held in Isolate into magicule on demand.

**Food Chain**

**Provide** grants one of your mastered skills to a subordinate. **Receive** permanently copies a mastered skill from a subordinate in return - a two-way bond.

**Soul Consume**

Hold to unleash a Soul Steal aura, instantly reaping the souls of nearby foes whose will has broken. Any hit landed while this mode is active also inflicts Soul Drain.

**How to Unlock**

Master **Gluttony** and **Merciless**, become a True Demon Lord or True Hero, and reach **10,000,000 EP**.
  
Learning Beelzebuth consumes **Merciless** as its price.
  
*Servers may relax these requirements via config.*

## Gaia, Lord of Earth

Type: Ultimate Skill
  
The land itself answers. Every other Ultimate travels with you unchanged; Gaia is only as strong as the ground beneath your feet is yours.

Evolves from **Earth Wall** and **Stone Shot** (both mastered), and asks that your nation already hold something worth defending. Neither magic is consumed, and learning it costs **no magicule**.

**Whose Ground**

Before anything else, Gaia asks where you stand.


- On your own nation's claim, everything strikes at **160%**.
- On unclaimed land, **60%**.

- Inside another nation's claim, **30%** - unless you are at war with them, and then the land forgets its loyalty and gives you **100%**.
  
Only the force, reach and duration bend this way. What you *pay* never changes.

**Tectonic Spikes**

A widening cone of crystal spikes tears up out of the ground ahead of you, throwing whatever they catch into the air.

**72** damage and **19 blocks** of reach on your own soil (a base 45 and 12, scaled by whose land you stand on - 27 and 7 in the wild, half that again on hostile ground). The spikes stand for **10 seconds** and then sink away on their own.

Cast over water or open air and nothing rises - and nothing is spent. Costs **3,000** magicule. **8s** cooldown.

**Stone Grasp**

Stone closes on one quarry within **32 blocks** and holds it where it stands - **8 seconds** on your own soil, 5 at war, less elsewhere - and crushes it in one blow as spikes ring its feet: **96** on your own soil, 60 at war, 36 in the wild, 18 on hostile ground.

It cannot walk away. It can still turn, still strike, still work its own arts - this binds the legs, not the will.
  
Costs **12,000** magicule. **35s** cooldown.

**Sculpt**

Raises or lowers a **3x3** patch of earth by one layer where you look - sneak to lower it. Unlike the spikes, this change is **permanent**.

It works *only* inside your own nation's claim, and only on plain earth and stone. It will not touch a chest, a machine, or anything another builder set down.
  
Costs **800** magicule. **2s** cooldown.

**Vein Bloom**

Coaxes ore out of the bare stone already beneath you - iron, copper and coal most often, gold rarer still, and diamond rarest of all.

This is a trade, not a gift. Each vein drawn out costs **400** of the *chunk's own* magicule, and a single patch of ground will yield no more than **12** veins in a day. Bleed the land dry and it gives nothing until it recovers.
  
Costs **5,000** magicule. **60s** cooldown.

**Geomancy: Seeding**

Pours up to **2,000** of your own magicule into the ground you stand on.

On your **own claim** it does more than fill: it deepens the land, raising how much that ground can ever hold by **250**. That deepening *stays*. Seed the same fields long enough and your nation stands on richer earth than anyone else's.

Elsewhere it merely tops the ground up and is forgotten. Nothing takes root near world spawn.
  
**10s** cooldown.

**Geomancy: Stripping**

Sneak instead, and you tear **250** of that depth back out and pocket what the land can no longer keep.

This works on *anyone's* ground, including a rival's carefully tended fields - but every strip that lowers a floor, your own included, burns **a fifth of your maximum magicule**. You will almost always end poorer than you began.
  
**10s** cooldown.

**Bulwark of Gaia**

A toggle apart from the modes. Standing on your own claim, you and every countryman within **12 blocks** are shielded by **Resistance II**.

Step off your land and the shield sleeps - but the earth keeps taking its **1,000** magicule each tick regardless. Lighting it is a decision, not a habit. It snuffs itself out the moment you cannot pay.

**Weight of the World**

The ground never harms its lord. You take **no falling damage**, ever, from any height.
  
*What you rule cannot wound you.*

## Hephaestus, Lord of Creation

Type: Ultimate Skill
  
The forge answers its lord. Where other Ultimates make you stronger in a fight, Hephaestus makes you stronger at the anvil - and then lets you carry the anvil into the fight.

Evolves from **Creator** and **Godly Craftsman** (both mastered). Neither is consumed, and learning it costs **no magicule**.

**Reforge**

Mends the item in your hand to whole. If it came from a Forge station, its quality is *rerolled* from nothing - a fresh score between **35** and **95**. The old worth is not remembered, for good or ill.

Costs **8,000** magicule, plus **4** more for every point of damage mended. **120s** cooldown.

**Engrave**

Opens the engraving choice for the held item and burns one mark into it.
  
Costs a flat **6,000** magicule, paid as the choice opens; the mark it burns is always level 1. **90s** cooldown.

**Conjure Arms**

Draws a blade out of nothing, bound to your soul. It lasts **5 minutes** and unmakes itself the moment you drop it.
  
Its quality climbs with your forging mastery - **one rank per 8 levels**. A novice conjures a novice's sword.

Costs **25,000** magicule. **10 minute** cooldown.

**Hammer of Creation**

A hammerfall in a **5 block** cone, striking everything before you.
  
Damage is **12** base, **+0.8** for each level of forging mastery, and **+6** for every quality rank of the finest thing you have ever made.

The smith's whole life swings with the hammer. Costs **4,000** magicule. **4s** cooldown.

**Chains of Hephaestus**

Binds the nearest quarry within a **12 block** cone where it stands, for **6 seconds**.
  
Costs **10,000** magicule. **30s** cooldown.

**Ore Sense**

A toggle apart from the modes. Every ore within **24 blocks** glows through solid stone - including magic ore mother rock.
  
It drains **150** magicule each tick and snuffs itself out with a warning the moment you cannot pay.

**Divine Hand**

Once per day, the first forge stage you play scores a flawless **100/100**. You still swing the hammer; the result was decided before you did.
  
*The forge guides your hand.*

**Nation Forge Boon**

While you are online and sworn to a nation, **every** online member of that nation forges better - a flat **+3 forge quality**, all of them, all the time.
  
It costs you nothing and has no daily limit. A nation with a Lord of Creation in it simply makes finer things than one without.

## Ouroboros, Lord of Eternity

Type: Ultimate Skill
  
The serpent that devours its own tail. Where Beelzebuth turns hunger outward, Ouroboros turns it inward - self-consumption that fuels endless renewal.

Evolves from **Survivor** and **Gourmet** (both mastered). Neither is consumed - the serpent takes nothing but its own tail.

**Eternal Renewal**

Toggle. While active, a share of the damage you deal returns to you as flesh and magicule - but your strikes land softer.
  
The serpent feeds on the fight itself. The icon shows while the feast lasts.

**Timeless Fangs**

Prime the fangs, then strike: your next blow bites three times - once now, twice more across the following seconds, each echo harder than the last.
  
The echoes pierce armor and belong to no one.

**Molt**

Shed your skin: severance and every affliction are cast off, and new scales harden over you - golden hearts that grow with what you shed.
  
Your own Cycle Debt clings too deep to molt away.

**Coil Back**

The coil remembers. One press returns you to where you stood five seconds ago and mends half of what you lost on the way.
  
The serpent does not undo the past; it slithers back along it.

**Cycle's End**

Pay half your greatest magicule to arm the cycle - the mark shows how long it holds. Die while it holds and you die in truth: the price is paid, and then you rise where you fell, diminished, bearing Cycle Debt that stacks with each rebirth.

A severed soul cannot complete the circle.

**Serpent's Maw**

The serpent's jaws close on everything before you: a bite that strikes all in a cone ahead, dealing heavy damage plus a share of each victim's greatest health - and a quarter of all it takes returns to you as flesh.

An empty bite costs nothing. The maw does not snap at air.

## Raphael, Lord of Wisdom

Type: Ultimate Skill

The Lord of Wisdom — a battlefield-computation entity. Power flows from **Analysis**: mark a target with Analyze, then Prediction can negate their attacks, the Multilayer Barrier hardens against them, and Reverse-Engineer reconstructs their skills — no luck involved.

**Shift-press** Analyze to cycle appraisal vision: Off, Entities, Blocks, Both.

**Independent Counsel**

Raphael watches the battle and **proposes** actions — deploying the barrier when your vitals drop, engaging Prediction when an analyzed hostile closes in. Press the skill key while a proposal is pending to approve it.

Against a lethal blow, Raphael acts on its own: an emergency defense leaves you at 1 HP, at a heavy magicule cost.

**Ars Magna &amp; Workshop**

Instant casting, chant annulment and halved magicule costs apply only while a **computed situation** exists — a live analysis, Prediction, or the barrier running.

The Sage's Workshop mode bundles the artisan facilities: spatial storage, research enchanting, refining, repeat crafting, Degenerate crafting and synthesis/separation (sneak to cycle).

**How to Unlock**

Master both **Great Sage** and **Godly Craftsman**, become a True Demon Lord or True Hero, and reach **10,000,000 EP**.
  
*Servers may relax these requirements via config.*

## Void Sovereign

Type: Ultimate Skill
  
Dominion over the void. Open a void domain, strike with a null slash, dash through space with Event Horizon, and collapse the souls of those caught within.
  
Evolves from Void Edge.

**How to Unlock**

Master **Void Edge** (itself only ever granted by an operator), become a True Demon Lord or True Hero, reach **10,000,000 EP**, and pay **50,000** magicule.
  
Learning Void Sovereign consumes Void Edge - the blade becomes the throne.

*Servers may relax these requirements via config.*
