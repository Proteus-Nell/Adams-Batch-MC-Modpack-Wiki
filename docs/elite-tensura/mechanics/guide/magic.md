# Magic

<small>[Elite Tensura](../../index.md) &rsaquo; [Elite Tensura Codex](index.md)</small>

Aspectual, spiritual, and summoning spells beyond the Skill system.

## Gravity Well

Type: Aspectual Magic (Gravity)
  
Hold to warp space where you are aiming - a gravity circle forms as you charge for **3 seconds** (**2s** once mastered) - then release to collapse it into a crushing sphere, up to **24 blocks** away.

**The Well**

For **8 seconds**, everything living inside the **6-block** sphere is dragged toward its centre - hardest at the rim, so clawing away only tightens the grip. Flight and dashes die at the edge.
  
Once a second the well crushes everyone inside for **3%** of their maximum health.

**Costs**

Costs **500 magicule** per cast. Cooldown: **25s**, or **18s** once mastered.
  
By default the well does not pull or crush players of your own nation.

**Charged Collapse**

Once mastered, keep holding past the normal charge time: **900 magicule** for a **9-block** sphere lasting **10 seconds** that crushes for **5%** of max health per second.

**How to Unlock**

Requires **Burden** magic mastered and at least **50,000 EP** - only a magician who has made weight itself a weapon can fold space into a well.

## Kamehameha

Type: Aspectual Magic (Misc)
  
Hold to gather energy - a magic circle forms as you charge for **3 seconds** (**2s** once mastered) - then release to fire a sustained blue-white energy wave. The beam follows your aim for **3 seconds**, reaching up to **40 blocks**.

**Costs &amp; Damage**

Costs **2,500 magicule** per cast. The wave deals **20** damage per hit and strikes repeatedly while a target stays inside it - sweep it across enemies or hold it on one for devastating total damage. Where it meets terrain it **explodes**.

Cooldown: **10s**, or **3s** once mastered.

**Charged Wave**

Once mastered, keep holding past the normal charge time to unleash the full Kamehameha: **5,000 magicule**, **35** damage per hit, a much thicker beam that burns for **5 seconds** with a far bigger explosion.

**How to Unlock**

Requires **Thunder Lance** magic mastered and **50,000 EP** - only a magician who has already shaped raw energy into a lance can hold a wave of this magnitude.

## Static Chain

Type: Aspectual Magic (Lightning)

Hold to cast, then release to fire a lightning arc at whatever you're looking at within **14 blocks**. The arc then jumps to nearby enemies - up to **4** targets total - dealing less damage with each hop, and leaving every target *Glowing* and *Slowed*.

**Costs &amp; Damage**

Costs **120 magicule** per cast. First hit deals **20** magic damage, each following hop dealing **65%** of the last.
  
Chain jumps reach up to **6 blocks** between targets.

**Charged Cast**

Once mastered, holding past the normal cast time charges the spell: **200 magicule**, **35** base damage, **4** extra hops, and each hop only decays to **80%** of the last - the arc holds its power much longer.

**How to Unlock**

Requires **Thunder Lance** mastered and **25,000 EP** - Static Chain builds on it.
