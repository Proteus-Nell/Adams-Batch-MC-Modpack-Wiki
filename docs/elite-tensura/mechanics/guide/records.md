# Records

<small>[Elite Tensura](../../index.md) &rsaquo; [Elite Tensura Codex](index.md)</small>

Permanent progression that outlasts death.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/tensura/item/orb_of_domination.png) Record Bonuses

Every earned Record grants a **permanent bonus** - and unlike titles, there are no slots to manage. All of your Record bonuses are *always active*.
  
Bonuses include:
- Attribute boosts
- Status effects
- Skill-mastery gains

**Ever Stronger**

Records come in seven rarities - Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, **Divine**.
  
The more Records you write, the permanently stronger you become - a reward no reset can take away.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/tensura/item/grimoire_a.png) Records

**Records** are a permanent chronicle of your journey. Once earned, a Record is yours *forever* - it survives prestige resets and even death.
  
Enabled by the **ETRecordsEnabled** gamerule (on by default).

**Records vs Titles**

Records mirror the Title system, but with one big difference - they are **never wiped** and never need equipping. Their rewards are simply always on.
  
They are grouped into categories:
- Combat &amp; Exploration
- Crafting &amp; Nation
- Social &amp; World Event

## ![](../../../assets/icons/tensura/item/high_quality_magic_crystal.png) Unlocking Records

Each Record lists **conditions** - feats the game tracks for you automatically. Meet every condition and the Record is written at once.
  
Examples:
- Kills, EP reached, deaths survived
- Biomes &amp; structures found, distance walked

- Skills learned/mastered, forge crafts
- Calamities slain, wars won, Walpurgis attended

**World-First Records**

Some Records are a race. The **first player ever** to achieve one claims the **World First** - announced to the entire server by the Voice of the World.
  
Everyone can still earn the Record afterward; only the first claims the glory.
