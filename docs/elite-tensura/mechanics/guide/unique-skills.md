# Unique Skills

<small>[Elite Tensura](../../index.md) &rsaquo; [Elite Tensura Codex](index.md)</small>

Rare Skills born of a singular will, often the seed of greater power.

## Brain

Type: Unique Skill

Brain gives a creature a working mind. Any mob holding this skill starts using everything it knows the way a player would - keeping its defensive skills switched on, pressing its combat skills when threatened, and casting its learned spells in battle.

**How It Fights**

Every few seconds the mob sizes up its situation. In a fight it hurls its strongest direct-damage magic at its target - fire, ice, lightning and the like come first, trickier control spells second. When it drops below **half health**, it switches to healing magic instead.

Spells cost the mob real magicule - when it runs dry, the casting stops.

**Reading the Signs**

A casting mob telegraphs: a **magic circle** appears above its head, colored to match the element it is about to unleash, and the spell launches from the circle itself. That flash is your window - interrupt it, shield up, or get out of the line of fire.

**How to Unlock**

Granted by an operator only, and meant for mobs, not players - on a player it simply sits inert. Use it to turn a named guard, boss, or pet into a genuine spellcaster: teach the mob skills and magic, then grant it Brain and let it think for itself.

## Crocodile Skin

Type: Unique Skill
  
Scaled hide that toughens as your EP grows, granting tiered armor while toggled on.
  
Evolves from Dragon Skin, and can itself grow toward greater skins.

**How to Unlock**

Master **Dragon Skin** and reach **1,000 EP**.
  
Learning Crocodile Skin consumes Dragon Skin.

## Elder Soul

**Elder Soul** is a primordial soul in full bloom - the Unique evolution of Ancient Soul.
  
Its aura reaches **40 blocks** (**50** mastered), granting allies **Strength III**, **Regeneration III** and **Resistance II**.

**Revealing Presence**

Every hostile creature - and enemy player - inside your aura is outlined with a glow *all* your allies can see, lingering a moment after they slip out.
  
Your Soul Sight reaches 50 blocks. Drain: 4 magicules/tick.

**How to Unlock**

Master **Ancient Soul**, reach **100,000 EP** and defeat **5 bosses** (Tensura bosses or world calamities), then learn it for 1,000 magicules.
  
Learning Elder Soul consumes Ancient Soul.

## Fortune's Eye

**Fortune's Eye** is the Scavenger's instinct honed into true sight - a Unique evolution.
  
Permanent **Luck III** (**Luck IV** mastered) - no toggle, always on.

**Loot Sense**

Press to reveal every dropped item within **24 blocks** (**40** mastered) with a glowing outline through walls, for 10 seconds.
  
Costs 100 magicules; cooldown 60 s (30 mastered).

**How to Unlock**

Master **Scavenger**, reach **100,000 EP** and defeat **5 bosses** (Tensura bosses or world calamities), then learn it for 1,000 magicules.
  
Learning Fortune's Eye consumes Scavenger.

## Hecate, Goddess of Magic

Type: Unique Skill
  
Hecate, goddess of magic. In your skill slot, nearly every spell you cast costs only **10%** of its normal magicule - Reincarnation is the one exception, kept at full price.

**Toggle**

Toggling Hecate on grants instant casting with no chant needed, faster skill learning and mastery gain, and **+30** bonus damage to all your spell damage (**+75** once mastered).
  
Draws **50 magicule** per tick while active - toggles off on its own if you run dry.

**How to Unlock**

Granted by an operator only - Hecate is not learned through normal growth.

## Stillness

**Stillness** is Meditation perfected - a Unique evolution.
  
**Serenity** (toggle): aura, magicule and spiritual-health regeneration at double Meditation's rate - triple once mastered - for 30 magicules/tick.

**Inner Calm**

Switch to the second mode and press: instantly restore **25%** of your MAX magicules and aura (**40%** mastered).
  
Cooldown 5 minutes (3 mastered). Free to cast.

**How to Unlock**

Master **Meditation**, reach **50,000 EP** and defeat **3 bosses** (Tensura bosses or world calamities), then learn it for 1,000 magicules.
  
Learning Stillness consumes Meditation.

## Unbreakable

**Unbreakable** is Iron Resolve tempered until it refuses to break - all four arts, sharpened:
- **Vein Sense** - Haste X
- **Iron Aegis** - up to **50%** damage reduction at full magicules
- **Resolute Surge** - Strength IV, 5 min (10 mastered)

**Undying Will**

Arm the fourth mode: a blow that would kill you is refused - you survive at **10%** health with 3 seconds of full immunity and an automatic Surge.
  
Once per **10 minutes** (6 mastered). The void and /kill ignore it.

**How to Unlock**

Master **Iron Resolve**, reach **150,000 EP** and defeat **8 bosses** (Tensura bosses or world calamities), then learn it for 1,000 magicules.
  
Learning Unbreakable consumes Iron Resolve.

## Void Edge

Type: Unique Skill
  
Cloak yourself in the void and hurl void projectiles at your enemies.
  
This skill is the precursor to the Void Sovereign ultimate.

**How to Unlock**

Granted by an operator or in Creative mode - it is not learned through normal growth.
  
Note: the Void Edge *sword* is a separate weapon item, not this skill.
