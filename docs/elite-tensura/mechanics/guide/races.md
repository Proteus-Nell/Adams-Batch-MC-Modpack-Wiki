## ![](../../../assets/icons/tensura/item/dragon_essence.png) Saiyan

The Saiyan bloodline - a warrior race that grows stronger with every battle and brush with death.
  
Saiyans ascend through four tiers of raw power:
- Lesser Saiyan
- Medium Saiyan
- High Saiyan
- Divine Saiyan

Each tier raises combat potential and unlocks greater strength.
