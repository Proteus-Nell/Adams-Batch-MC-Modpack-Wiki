# Forge

<small>[Elite Tensura](../../index.md) &rsaquo; [Elite Tensura Codex](index.md)</small>

Craft legendary gear at the forge.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/elitetensura/item/aetherforged_katana.png) Aetherforged Gear

**Aetherforged** gear is Star-Iron - near-black plate veined with cyan light, forged from Hihiirokane under conditions only a **Dragonforge** station can reach.

Four weapons and a four-piece armor set. Every piece comes only from the forge, so quality, engravings and the minigame all apply.
  
*Nothing here needs a skill or race - if you can run a Dragonforge, you can wear it.*

**The Weapons**

Each weapon carries one **signature active** on right-click, paid in aura and magicule:
- **Spear** - *Piercing Vector*: hold to charge (up to 1.5s), release to lunge and thrust 6 blocks for 1.5x-3x your attack. 300/300, 8s

- **Katana** - *Phase Cut*: arms the blade for 5s; the next hit adds half your attack as armor-ignoring phase damage. 250/250, 10s
- **War Hammer** - *Crushing Impact*: slam a 4-block ring for 1.2x attack, knock-up and Slowness II. 400/400, 12s

- **Gauntlets** - *Aether Burst*: a 3.5-block shockwave for 0.8x attack that hurls everything outward. 200/200, 8s

**Cooldown Panel**

Every Aetherforged weapon, plus the Void Edge and Astral Edge swords, reports to one **Gear Cooldowns** panel above your hotbar. Gear in your hands is listed in white with every mode; gear stowed in the hotbar gets one grey line each, soonest ready first.

Each row shows the item, the ability name, a bar that fills as the cooldown runs down, and the seconds left - or **READY**.
  
Nothing is drawn when you carry none of this gear. Move the panel from the mod list's **Config** button, under HUD Positions.

**Aetherforged Plate**

The armor sits a full step above Hihiirokane in every number - defense, toughness, knockback resistance and durability - and every piece is a fresh canvas for forge engravings.

Wear all **four** pieces and the set answers your power: incoming damage is cut by up to **25%**, scaling with your EP and reaching its peak at ten million.
  
The gauntlets are worn, not held - they show as bracers on your arms.

**Forging It**

All recipes are **Dragonforge** tier:
- Each armor piece - the matching Hihiirokane piece + 2 Hihiirokane ingots + 2 Orichalcum ingots
- Spear / Katana - the Hihiirokane spear or katana + 2 Hihiirokane + 2 Orichalcum

- War Hammer - 4 Hihiirokane + 2 Pure Magisteel + 2 Earth cores
- Gauntlets - 4 Hihiirokane + 2 Pure Magisteel + 2 Space cores
  
The finished Hihiirokane input is consumed - you are reforging it, not copying it.

Every recipe also needs **Star Iron**: 4 for each weapon, 3 for chestplate/leggings, 2 for helmet/boots. It only falls during a **Starfall** event - see that entry.

## Engravings

Twenty-eight custom **engravings** join the base engraving pool - 22 beneficial and 6 **cursed**. Gear earns them three ways:
- **EP growth** - when a piece crosses a major EP threshold and a Rare or Epic engraving would roll, there is a 25% chance it becomes a custom one instead

- **Forge crafting** - quality crafts come pre-engraved from a much larger pool
- **Curses** - half of the game's own curse rolls, and forge crafts that fire two critical upgrades, may pick up a cursed engraving

**EP Rolls: Armor**

The roll must be one of the game's Rare or Epic engravings; the item's **slot** then decides the pool. On **armor** it can become:
- **Magicule Conduit** - +25% magicule regen per level (max 4)
- **Aura Conduit** - +25% aura regen per level (max 4)

- **Featherfall** - -25% fall damage per level, boots (max 2)

**EP Rolls: Weapons**

On **hand-held gear** it can become:
- **Frostbrand** - slows on hit (max 4)
- **Vampiric** - heal 8% of damage per level (max 5)
- **Voidpiercer** - bonus damage scaling with the target's armor (max 5)

- **Executioner** - +10%/level vs targets under 30% health (max 3)
- **Rending** - shreds 2 armor per level for a few seconds (max 3)

**Forge-Only**

Fourteen more only come from the forge: **Keen Edge**, **Featherweight**, **Reinforced**, **Toughened**, **Vitality**, **Swiftness**, and **Regeneration** (self-repair).

The rest: **Critical Strike**, **Soulbound** (kept on death), **Soul Render** (vs spiritual), **Stormbrand** (lightning arcs), **Predator's Mark** (+kill EP), **Cleave** (splash hits), and **Prospector** (duplicate drops).

**Cursed**

Six **cursed** engravings trade a real drawback for a real edge - one curse per item at most, never above level 1, and a grindstone will not strip them:
- **Blood Pact** - +20% melee damage, each hit costs 2 HP (never lethal)

- **Berserker's Brand** - +30% melee below half health, +25% damage taken
- **Mana Thirst** - +20% melee while each hit drains 250 magicule; -20% when you cannot pay
- **Iron Maiden** - +4 armor +3 toughness, -15% speed
- **Parasite Skin** - +10 max HP +50% magicule regen, healing -75%

- **Thorn Pact** - reflect 15% melee, +10% damage taken

**From the Forge**

Quality forge work fills its engraving slots automatically - **Superior 1**, **Masterwork 2**, **Legendary 3**, **Mythical 4** - matched to the item: weapons get weapon engravings, armor gets armor engravings. Better minigame scores mean higher engraving levels.

Craft engravings survive gear evolution and stack with ones earned later through EP.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/tensura/item/great_sword_schematic.png) Recipes &amp; Enhancements

Each forge recipe defines its **ingredients**, the minimum station **tier** it needs, which **minigames** you must clear, and what it produces.

Recipes are **data-driven**: packs can add or tweak them, and they hot-reload with */reload*. Some stay hidden until discovered.

**Quality**

Every craft earns a **Quality** rank from how well you forge:
- Poor, Common, Fine, Superior, Masterwork
- Legendary &amp; Mythical - only via lucky *critical upgrades*

Higher quality means stronger stats, more durability, and more **engraving slots** (Superior 1 -&gt; Mythical 4).

**Engravings**

Each engraving slot is filled with an **engraving** matched to the item - weapons draw from the weapon pool, armor from the armor pool. See the **Engravings** entry for the full list.

Slot count comes from quality, plus extras from your skills and titles. Better minigame scores mean **higher engraving levels**.
  
Gear forged before this system converts automatically and keeps its power.

**Boosting Your Craft**

Your quality, slot count, and levels improve with:
- A high minigame **score**
- Crafting skills like *Godly Craftsman*
- Titles, synergies, and a forge **specialization** (weaponsmith, armorsmith...)
  
Admins manage recipes with */etadmin forge*.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/elitetensura/item/ancient_runic_sword.png) Runesmith's Insight

**Runesmith's Insight** is the smith's own skill - the one thing at the forge you cannot buy, trade, or learn with Existence Points.

It arrives on its own. Finish **25** successful crafts and it is simply yours, with no prompt and no cost. Nothing you do at the anvil is wasted; the count only ever goes up.

**At the Anvil**

From the moment you hold it, every forging minigame gets kinder.


- The sweet spot is **25%** wider, so the band you are aiming for is easier to hit.
- Heat drains **25%** slower, giving you more time before the temperature drifts out from under you.

Both apply the instant a craft begins. There is nothing to switch on.

**Mastery**

Every successful forge after you learn it adds one mastery point. Keep working and it masters itself.

A **mastered** Runesmith's Insight gives each craft a **25%** chance of one *extra engraving slot* - a free roll at another property on the finished piece, on top of everything your other skills grant.

It stacks with Godly Craftsman, Researcher, Analyst and the rest. They are not alternatives to each other.

**Notes**

The skill sits in your list but has no button and no active use - it works entirely on its own while you craft.
  
Forging is on by default. If an admin has turned it off with the **ETForgeEnabled** gamerule, nothing at the anvil progresses, this skill included.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/elitetensura/item/engraving_scroll.png) Salvage &amp; Reroll

Every Forge Station has a **Salvage** button next to Specialize. It works on any forged item except Aetherforged gear and Conjured items - no skill needed.

This is separate from Hephaestus's Reforge and Engrave, which stay skill-gated. Salvage and Reroll are open to everyone at the station.

**Salvaging**

Salvage destroys the item and refunds part of its recipe's ingredients - **20%** at Poor up to **75%** at Mythical. Tag ingredients refund nothing.
  
Weapon Legend kill history is lost with the item.

Each engraving on it has a **25%** chance to yield one **Engraving Scroll** capturing that engraving and its level, up to one scroll per salvage. Cursed engravings are never captured.

**Engraving Scroll**

Off-hand a forged item with a free slot and use the scroll: it applies the captured engraving at its level and is consumed.
  
Refuses a full item, an Aetherforged/Conjured item, or an engraving it can't hold.

**Rerolling**

Reroll swaps one engraving for a different random one at the same level, for materials (about a quarter of the recipe) plus cash on banking-enabled servers, scaled by rank.

Capped at **3 rerolls** per item - the count survives a Hephaestus Reforge. Rerolling never touches Weapon Legend.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/tensura/item/high_magisteel_ingot.png) The Forge

The Forge lets you craft powerful, one-of-a-kind gear by hand rather than on a crafting table.
  
Interact with a Forge Station block to open the forge and choose a recipe.

Enabled by the ETForgeEnabled gamerule (on by default).

Craft a **Basic Forge Station** from 8 iron ingots around an anvil. Place it, then right-click to open the forge.

*Recipe:* `elitetensura:forge_station_basic`

**Station Upgrades**

A station levels up on its own as you complete crafts on it:
- Basic - **20** crafts -&gt; Magisteel
- Magisteel - **40** crafts -&gt; Mythril
- Mythril - **80** crafts -&gt; Dragonforge

On reaching the count, the station upgrades in place with a flourish and its craft counter resets.
  
Higher tiers can craft everything the lower tiers can, and they raise the minigame difficulty for tougher gear.

*Server-configurable - a threshold of 0 disables that upgrade step.*

**Nation Bonuses**

A station in your nation's own territory needs fewer crafts to upgrade, the discount growing with your nation's level.
  
The **Forge Boon** nation perk goes further, raising the quality and crit chance of every craft made inside that territory.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/tensura/item/magic_stone.png) The Minigame

Forging is a minigame. Each recipe runs you through challenges you must clear well to craft a quality result:
- Hammer Timing - strike on rhythm
- Dragonfire Forging - work the bellows to keep the heat in the hot band, then strike
- Rune Alignment - line up the runes

- Magicule Flow - steady the flow of power (this one drains real magicule up front)

The better you perform across each challenge, the higher the quality of the gear you forge. Rush it and the result suffers.

Score also feeds **critical upgrades** - a perfect run adds up to +15% crit chance - and sets a floor: score **90+** guarantees at least Fine, **98+** at least Superior. Difficulty follows the *recipe* tier, not the station.

## ![](../../../assets/icons/tensura/item/hihiirokane_katana.png) Weapon Legend

Forged weapons remember. Every hostile creature or player you slay with a forged weapon in hand is carved into its history - and the weapon grows a **legend**.
  
Passive animals don't count. Neither does anything killed bare-handed.

**Legend Tiers**

- **Blooded** - 100 kills: the weapon's story appears in its tooltip
- **Renowned** - 1,000 kills: a ✯ joins its name
- **Legendary** - 10,000 kills: the name turns gold with ✯✯
  
Thresholds are server-configurable.

**Slayer of Giants**

Boss kills - the world's counted bosses and this server's calamity horrors - are tallied separately.
  
At **25 boss kills** the weapon earns **one extra engraving**, chosen by fate. Once per weapon, ever.

A legend never fades: reforging and gear evolution keep the tally.
  
Anvil names are respected - your "Cabbage Slicer" stays the Cabbage Slicer, stars and all in its tooltip.
