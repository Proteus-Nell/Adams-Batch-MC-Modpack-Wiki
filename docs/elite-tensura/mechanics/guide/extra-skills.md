# Extra Skills

<small>[Elite Tensura](../../index.md) &rsaquo; [Elite Tensura Codex](index.md)</small>

Refined techniques that sharpen body, mind, and fortune.

## Ancient Soul

Type: Extra Skill
  
Toggle on to bathe nearby allies in an ancient aura - Strength III, Regeneration III, and Resistance I - while you gain Soul Sight to read the souls around you.

Drains magicule while active. Learning it demands great EP and 25 resets.

**How to Unlock**

Reach **5,000 EP** and have reset at least **25 times**.

**Evolution:** master Ancient Soul, reach **100,000 EP** and defeat **5 bosses** to evolve it into **Elder Soul** - a far wider aura that also reveals every enemy inside it.

## Iron Resolve

Type: Extra Skill
  
A four-mode discipline of body and will:
- Vein Sense - Haste
- Iron Aegis - damage reduction scaled to EP
- Resolute Surge - Strength
- Last Stand - endure a killing blow

**How to Unlock**

Granted by an operator or in Creative mode.

**Evolution:** master Iron Resolve, reach **150,000 EP** and defeat **8 bosses** to evolve it into **Unbreakable** - a 50% Aegis, Strength IV Surge, and a once-per-cooldown refusal to die.

## Meditation

Type: Extra Skill
  
Center yourself to accelerate the regeneration of aura, magicule, and spiritual health.
  
Toggle on to begin; it slowly drains magicule while active.

**How to Unlock**

Reach **500 EP**.

**Evolution:** master Meditation, reach **50,000 EP** and defeat **3 bosses** to evolve it into **Stillness** - doubled regeneration and an instant recovery burst.

## Scavenger

Type: Extra Skill
  
Fortune favors you. Passively grants Luck II.
  
Once mastered, toggle the skill on to raise your fortune to Luck III.

**How to Unlock**

Granted by an operator or in Creative mode.
  
Once held, its passive Luck works for everyone; only mastered holders can toggle the Luck III boost.

**Evolution:** master Scavenger, reach **100,000 EP** and defeat **5 bosses** to evolve it into **Fortune's Eye** - permanent Luck III and a loot-revealing pulse.
