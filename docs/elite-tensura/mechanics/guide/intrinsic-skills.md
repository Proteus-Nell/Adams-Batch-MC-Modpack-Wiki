# Intrinsic Skills

<small>[Elite Tensura](../../index.md) &rsaquo; [Elite Tensura Codex](index.md)</small>

Innate, body-born abilities. The two Mechanical skills require the Create mod to be installed.

## Kaioken

Type: Intrinsic Skill

A Saiyan's defiant technique - burn your life force to multiply your power in a blazing crimson aura. While it holds, your **magicule and aura** swell far past their limit and your strength, armor and speed surge with them. The red aura wraps your whole body in flame.

**Ranks**

Kaioken has four ranks, each a greater multiplier:
- **Kaioken** - double energy
- **Kaioken x3**
- **Kaioken x10**
- **Kaioken x20**

Higher ranks open as your **EP** grows: x3 at **100,000**, x10 at **1,000,000**, and x20 at **10,000,000** - and only once the skill is **mastered**. Cycle to the rank you can bear before you ignite.

The aura itself tells the tale: deep blood red at first, then orange, then gold, and white-hot at x20 - anyone watching can read how far you have pushed.

**The Toll**

Power like this devours you. While Kaioken burns it drains your **health and energy** every moment, though never enough on its own to kill you. It lasts up to **3 minutes** - **6** once mastered - then burns out.

**Exhaustion**

When the aura fades your body pays: **Weakness, Fragility and Paralysis** for about **10 minutes**. You **cannot** ignite Kaioken again until that exhaustion clears, and it will not stack with any other transformation.

**How to Unlock**

Kaioken is not learned - it is **born into you**. It comes to **Medium Saiyans** and the greater Saiyan forms beyond, with no cost to acquire.

## Mechanical Eyes

Type: Intrinsic Skill
  
Requires the Create mod to be installed.
  
Your eyes gain the sight of Create's engineer's goggles, revealing stress and contraption information.

**How to Unlock**

Requires the **Create** mod to be installed.
  
With Create present, reach **25,000 EP**.

## Mechanical Hands

Type: Intrinsic Skill
  
Requires the Create mod to be installed.
  
Your hands act as a Create wrench. Two modes let you rotate or remove any wrenchable contraption block.

**How to Unlock**

Requires the **Create** mod to be installed.
  
With Create present, reach **25,000 EP**.
