## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +200 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for each use of the «Wormhole» ability, +1 experience point for every 50 blocks to the teleportation point.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in cave biomes, mineshaft chests.

### Wormhole

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Upon use, teleports the owner to their spawn point if it is within **500-1000 (up to 3000-6000)** blocks, then goes on cooldown for **120-60 (up to 60-30)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distance | 500-1000 | +50% of base per level | 3000-6000 |
| Cooldown | 120-60 | -5% of base per level | 60-30 |
