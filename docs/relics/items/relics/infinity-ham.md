## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **20**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

> A mysterious piece of meat that smoothly regenerates to its original size, no matter how much you carve it.

Found in loot chests of kind: village and pillager chests.

### Regeneration

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Every **30-15 (up to 25-10)** seconds, regenerates up to 6 edible chunks, each restoring **1-3 (up to 11-13)** hunger points. When consumed, the relic automatically uses the required number of chunks to replenish the player's hunger.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooldown | 30-15 | -0.5 per level | 25-10 |
| Feed | 1-3 | +1 per level | 11-13 |

### Marinade

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 10.*

Allows applying potion effects to the relic by left-clicking it with a potion in the inventory. Each time the relic is consumed, it applies these effects to the player for **1-3 (up to 3-9)** seconds per hunger point restored. Using a water bottle clears the last applied potion effects.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duration | 1-3 | +20% of base per level | 3-9 |

### Meat Bat

*Unlocks at relic level 10, passive. Ability max level 10.*

Allows the relic to be used as a melee weapon, dealing **0.5-2 (up to 1.8-7)** damage and stunning the target for **0.05-0.25 (up to 0.1-0.5)** seconds per chunk. Consumes all chunks when attacking, regardless of the target's remaining health.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage | 0.5-2 | +25% of base per level | 1.8-7 |
| Stun | 0.05-0.25 | +10% of base per level | 0.1-0.5 |
