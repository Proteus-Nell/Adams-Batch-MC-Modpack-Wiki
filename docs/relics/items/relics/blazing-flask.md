## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for every 5 seconds of flight within the scope of the ability «Eternal Fire».

Found in loot chests of kind: any Nether chest, fire/Nether-themed chests (and ruined portals).

### Eternal fire

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Using the ability on a block creates a restricted flight zone with a maximum flight speed of **0.1-0.4 (up to 1.6-8)** blocks per second and a height of **3-5 (up to 13-15)** blocks. The area size depends on the amount of fire within a radius of **1-2.5 (up to 2-5)** blocks around the center, but if there is no fire at all, the zone will not be created.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Step | 1-2.5 | +10% of base per level | 2-5 |
| Speed | 0.1-0.4 | +190% of base per level | 1.6-8 |
| Height | 3-5 | +1 per level | 13-15 |
