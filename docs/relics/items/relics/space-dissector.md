## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +200 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for each created portal if there is no existing one

Found in loot chests of kind: fire/Nether-themed chests (and ruined portals), any Nether chest.

### Dissection

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Holding RMB creates a portal at a point along the line of sight. Releasing RMB within a distance of **16-32 (up to 56-112)** blocks creates a second portal in the same way, linking it to the first. When a living entity approaches a portal, it is teleported to the linked portal. Portals do not function if impassable blocks are in their way. After **5-10 (up to 30-60)** seconds, the portals close and disappear.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distance | 16-32 | +25% of base per level | 56-112 |
| Time | 5-10 | +50% of base per level | 30-60 |
