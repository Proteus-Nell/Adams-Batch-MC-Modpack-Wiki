## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +200 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1/5/10 experience point for each killed Zombified Piglin / Piglin / Piglin Brute.  
+1 experience point for each additional trade from the ability «Respect».

Found in loot chests of kind: bastion chests.

### Recognition

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Makes piglins neutral in relation to the wearer. The nearest piglin will indicate the location of a bastion nearby.

### Respect

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 10.*

Each trade with piglins has a multiple 50% chance to yield an additional trade result, but the chance cannot trigger more than **0-1 (up to 10-11)** times.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rolls | 0-1 | +1 per level | 10-11 |
