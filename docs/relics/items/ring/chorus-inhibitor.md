## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for every 10 blocks to the teleportation point from the «Teleportation Control» ability.

Found in loot chests of kind: any End chest, End and stronghold chests.

### Teleportation Control

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Using a chorus fruit teleports the wearer to a block along the line of sight within **16-32 (up to 48-96)** blocks, then goes on cooldown for **5-10 (up to 0-5)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distance | 16-32 | +20% of base per level | 48-96 |
| Cooldown | 5-10 | -0.5 per level | 0-5 |
