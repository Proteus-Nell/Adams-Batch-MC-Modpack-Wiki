## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

> Condenses matter beneath the relic owner's feet, creating a path through any terrain, even where no solid ground exists.

Found in loot chests of kind: any End chest, End and stronghold chests.

### Phantom Bridge

*Active (toggleable). Ability max level 10.*

Creates a temporary phantom bridge beneath the player's feet that any relic owner can walk on. If the player stands still on the bridge for more than **0.2-1 (up to 1.5-6)** seconds, they will fall through and cannot get back up until they reach solid ground. While sneaking, the player is forced to fall through the phantom bridge blocks.
