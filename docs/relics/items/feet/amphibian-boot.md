## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for each second of acceleration from the «Fins» ability.  
+1 experience point for every second of acceleration from the «Slipping» ability.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in ocean/river biomes.

### Fins

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Increases swimming speed by **2-4 (up to 7-14)**% per second of swimming. The modifier stops increasing if the swimming duration exceeds **3-7 (up to 9-21)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed | 2-4 | +25% of base per level | 7-14 |
| Duration | 3-7 | +20% of base per level | 9-21 |

### Slipping

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Increases movement speed in the rain by **2-4 (up to 5-10)**% per second of movement. The modifier stops increasing if the duration of movement in the rain exceeds **3-5 (up to 7.5-12.5)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed | 2-4 | +15% of base per level | 5-10 |
| Duration | 3-5 | +15% of base per level | 7.5-12.5 |

### Gills

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

With a **1-10 (up to 4.5-45)**% chance, the player may not consume an air unit while swimming or in other conditions.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chance | 1-10 | +35% of base per level | 4.5-45 |
