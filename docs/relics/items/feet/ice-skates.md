## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +200 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for each second of acceleration from the ability «Skating».

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in snowy biomes.

### Skating

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Increases movement speed on ice by **10-35 (up to 25-87)**% per second of sliding. The modifier stops increasing if the sliding duration exceeds **2.5-5 (up to 5-10)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed | 10-35 | +15% of base per level | 25-87 |
| Duration | 2.5-5 | +10% of base per level | 5-10 |

### Ram

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 10.*

Upon collision with targets, deals **0.5-2.5 (up to 1-5)** damage per second of sliding from the «Skating» ability.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage | 0.5-2.5 | +10% of base per level | 1-5 |
