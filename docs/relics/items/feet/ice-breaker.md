## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +200 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for every 3 blocks in the fall before triggering the ability «Earthquake».

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in taiga biomes, chests in snowy biomes, chests in mountain biomes.

### Sustainability

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Increases fall speed, boosts knockback resistance by **20-50 (up to 80-100)**%, and removes sliding on blocks.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modifier | 20-50 | -90 per level | 80-100 |

### Earthquake

*Active (instantaneous). Ability max level 10.*

Prolonged use of the ability while falling, up until landing, will create a shockwave with a radius of **2.5-5 (up to 10-20)** blocks, dealing up to **2.5-5 (up to 8.8-17.5)** damage and knocking back nearby targets. However, the power of the shockwave also depends on the distance the wearer has fallen.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Size | 2.5-5 | +30% of base per level | 10-20 |
| Damage | 2.5-5 | +25% of base per level | 8.8-17.5 |
