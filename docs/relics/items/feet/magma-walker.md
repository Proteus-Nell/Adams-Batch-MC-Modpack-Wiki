## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +200 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for every 5 units of heating from the ability «Fiery Tread».

Found in loot chests of kind: any Nether chest, fire/Nether-themed chests (and ruined portals).

### Heat Resistance

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Suppresses damage from hot blocks.

### Fiery Tread

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

For **20-50 (up to 40-100)** seconds, allows walking on the surface of lava without sinking or taking damage. After the time runs out, the boots start overheating and deal damage to the wearer proportional to the level of overheating. To cool them down, the wearer must stay away from lava blocks. The cooling time also depends on the level of overheating.
