## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

> Grants the relic owner special elasticity, allowing them to bounce off any solid surface.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in mountain biomes.

### Elasticity

*Active (toggleable). Ability max level 10.*

Increases the elasticity of all blocks by **25-50 (up to 50-100)**% compared to slime blocks, enabling the player to bounce off them upon landing. When activated on the ground, it launches the player into the air.

| Power | 25-50 | +10% of base per level | 50-100 |
