## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +200 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for each second of acceleration from the ability «Acceleration».

Found in loot chests of kind: any Overworld chest.

### Acceleration

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Increases movement speed by **10-50 (up to 20-130)**% per second of movement. The modifier stops increasing if the movement duration exceeds **3-7 (up to 9-21)** seconds. All blocks also become slippery for the wearer.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed | 10-50 | +15% of base per level | 20-130 |
| Duration | 3-7 | +20% of base per level | 9-21 |
