## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for every 5 units of getting wet from the ability «Moisture Resistance».

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in ocean/river biomes.

### Moisture Resistance

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

For a certain period, allows walking on the surface of water without sinking. Once the time runs out, the boots start sinking, pulling the wearer underwater. To dry them, the wearer must stay away from water blocks. The drying time fully depends on the level of saturation.

|---|---|---|---|
| Time | 30-60 | +10% of base per level | 60-120 |
