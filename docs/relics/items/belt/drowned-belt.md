## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for each level of enchantment «Riptide» on the trident when using it.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in ocean/river biomes.

### Load Capacity

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Increases the maximum number of amulet equipment slots by +**0-2 (up to 10-12)**.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Charm | 0-2 | +1 per level | 10-12 |

### Dead Weight

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Reduces swimming speed by **50-25 (up to 0)**% and increases the wearer's sinking speed by **50-25 (up to 0)**%.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slowness | 50-25 | -5 per level | 0 |
| Sinking | 500-300 | -10 per level | 400-200 |

### Water Flows

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Increases the damage dealt by the player underwater by **125-200 (up to 250-400)**%.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage | 125-200 | +10% of base per level | 250-400 |

### Water Attraction

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Allows the trident's «Riptide» enchantment ability to be used without rain or water, adding a cooldown of **10-5 (up to 5-0)** seconds per enchantment level.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooldown | 10-5 | -0.5 per level | 5-0 |
