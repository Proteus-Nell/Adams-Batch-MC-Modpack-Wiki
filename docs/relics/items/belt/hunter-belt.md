## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for each attack made by the wearer's pet.

Found in loot chests of kind: any Overworld chest.

### Load Capacity

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Increases the maximum number of amulet equipment slots by +**1-2 (up to 11-12)**.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Charm | 1-2 | +1 per level | 11-12 |

### Training

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Increases the damage dealt by the wearer's pets by **125-200 (up to 312-500)**%.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage | 125-200 | +15% of base per level | 312-500 |
