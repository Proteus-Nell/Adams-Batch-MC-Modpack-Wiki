## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **20**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

> Grants the wearer the ability to turn enemies' healing against them.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in desert biomes.

### Faith

*Active (instantaneous). Ability max level 10.*

Has 2 toggleable modes: Holiness and Unholiness. In the red Holiness mode, steals **10-25 (up to 20-50)**% of the healing from visible entities within a radius of **5-10 (up to 17.5-35)** blocks and transfers it to the relic's wearer. In the blue Unholiness mode, deals damage to all visible entities within the same radius equal to **25-75 (up to 150-450)**% of the healing received by the relic's wearer.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health | 10-25 | +10% of base per level | 20-50 |
| Damage | 25-75 | +50% of base per level | 150-450 |
| Radius | 5-10 | +25% of base per level | 17.5-35 |

### Penitence

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 10.*

Ignites undead enemies for 10 seconds and increases damage dealt to them by **25-50 (up to 100-200)**%.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amount | 25-50 | +30% of base per level | 100-200 |

### Ascension

*Unlocks at relic level 10, passive. Ability max level 5.*

Killing a target grants the relic's wearer a temporary stacking immortality effect for **0.5-1 (up to 1-2)** seconds, up to a maximum of **7.5-15 (up to 30-60)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max Duration | 7.5-15 | x1.32 per level | 30-60 |
| Duration | 0.5-1 | +20% of base per level | 1-2 |
