## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +200 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for every 10 damage accumulated in the internal buffer of the relic from the ability «Infernal Hatred».

Found in loot chests of kind: fire/Nether-themed chests (and ruined portals), any Nether chest.

### Infernal Hatred

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Accumulates damage taken by the wearer into the relic's internal buffer, with a capacity of **20-60 (up to 90-270)** units. Five seconds after the last damage received or if the buffer overflows, the relic explodes, scattering obsidian shards in all directions. Each shard deals **0.25-0.5 (up to 1.5-3)** damage and stuns for **0.05-0.25 (up to 0.17-0.88)** seconds per unit of damage stored in the relic's internal buffer.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 20-60 | +35% of base per level | 90-270 |
| Damage | 0.25-0.5 | +50% of base per level | 1.5-3 |
| Stun | 0.05-0.25 | +25% of base per level | 0.17-0.88 |
