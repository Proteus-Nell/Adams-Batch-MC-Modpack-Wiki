## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +200 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for each activation of the ability «Electric Discharge».

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in ocean/river biomes.

### Power over the sea

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

The wearer does not sink in water.

### Electrical Discharge

*Active (toggleable). Ability max level 10.*

Deals **0.5-2.5 (up to 1.5-7.5)** damage to targets upon collision with them.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage | 0.5-2.5 | +20% of base per level | 1.5-7.5 |

### Paralysis

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 10.*

When the «Electric Discharge» ability activates, it applies paralysis to the target for **0.5-1.5 (up to 1-3)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duration | 0.5-1.5 | +10% of base per level | 1-3 |
