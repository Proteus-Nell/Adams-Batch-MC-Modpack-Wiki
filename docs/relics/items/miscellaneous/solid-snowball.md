## What it does

A packed snowball made with the [Wool Mitten](../hands/wool-mitten.md), which can keep packing more snow into it. Throw it with right-click. If you're wearing the Wool Mitten when it hits, it deals damage and **stuns** the target, both scaling with the snowball's size and the mitten's level. Without the mitten it does nothing. It's freezing to carry: unless you wear the Wool Mitten, holding one in your inventory slowly freezes you, as if you stood in powder snow. It stacks to 1.
