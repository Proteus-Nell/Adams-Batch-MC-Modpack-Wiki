## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for every 10 seconds of burning fuel when placing it in the internal buffer of the relic from the ability «Acceleration».

Found in loot chests of kind: any End chest, End and stronghold chests.

### Acceleration

*Active (cyclical). Ability max level 10.*

Using any combustible fuel that would work in a furnace on the relic in the inventory replenishes its internal buffer, which has a maximum capacity of **50-100 (up to 175-350)** units. Activating the ability while flying with elytra gradually increases the player's flight speed up to **17.6-24 (up to 35.2-48)** blocks per second, consuming fuel from the internal buffer.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 50-100 | +25% of base per level | 175-350 |
| Speed | 17.6-24 | +10% of base per level | 35.2-48 |
