## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for every 2 units of damage when attacking while being invisible from the «Disappearing» ability.

Found in loot chests of kind: any End chest, End and stronghold chests.

### Vanish

*Active (toggleable). Ability max level 10.*

Grants the wearer complete invisibility and increases their movement speed by **10-35 (up to 45-157)**% when the total light level does not exceed **1-2 (up to 11-12)**.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 1-2 | +1 per level | 11-12 |
| Speed | 10-35 | +35% of base per level | 45-157 |

### Betrayal

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

An attack while using the 'Vanish' ability deals **20-80 (up to 150-250)**% increased damage, marking a circle with a radius of **20-15 (up to 15-10)** blocks around the target and dispelling the wearer's invisibility. The 'Vanish' ability can only be used again after the wearer exits the circle.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage | 20-80 | +10% of base per level | 150-250 |
| Distance | 20-15 | -0.5 per level | 15-10 |
