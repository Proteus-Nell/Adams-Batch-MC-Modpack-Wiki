## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **15**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

> A masterfully forged, capricious weapon. If its temper is favorable, neither horde nor legion will withstand the onslaught of its thousand blades.

Found in loot chests of kind: any End chest, End and stronghold chests.

### Mayhem

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Dealing damage has a **5-15 (up to 8-25)**% chance to trigger a projectile that bounces chaotically between nearby targets within a 16-block radius, up to **2-4 (up to 5-10)** times, dealing **10-20 (up to 25-50)**% of the triggering attack's damage.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chance | 5-15 | +6.5% of base per level | 8-25 |
| Bounces | 2-4 | +15% of base per level | 5-10 |
| Damage | 10-20 | +15% of base per level | 25-50 |

### Cloning

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 10.*

Each projectile bounce from the Mayhem ability has a **5-10 (up to 10-20)**% chance to spawn an additional identical projectile.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chance | 5-10 | +10% of base per level | 10-20 |
