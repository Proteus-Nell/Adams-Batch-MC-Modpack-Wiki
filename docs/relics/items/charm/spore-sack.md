## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

> A simple burlap sack filled to the brim with deadly miasma.

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in jungle/tropical biomes.

### Spore Mist

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

When the player's health drops below 50%, releases up to **3-8 (up to 8-20)** homing spores around them. These spores deal damage equal to **10-25 (up to 30-75)**% of the player's missing health and block healing for affected targets for 5 seconds. The ability can trigger again once the player's health rises above 50%.
