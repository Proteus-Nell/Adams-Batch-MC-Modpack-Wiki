## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for using the ability «End Transposition», +1 experience point for every 10 blocks from the distance to the target when using.

Found in loot chests of kind: any End chest, End and stronghold chests.

### Neutrality

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Makes endermen neutral to the wearer.

### End Transposition

*Active (instantaneous). Ability max level 10.*

Swaps the user and the target along the line of sight within a range of up to **16-32 (up to 40-80)** blocks.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distance | 16-32 | +15% of base per level | 40-80 |
