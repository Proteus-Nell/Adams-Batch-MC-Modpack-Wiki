## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **20**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for every 3 charges from the ability «Berserker Rage» when they are reset.  
+1 experience point for each target hit by the «Spurt» ability.

Found in loot chests of kind: fire/Nether-themed chests (and ruined portals), any Nether chest.

### Berserker Rage

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Each attack made by the wearer within **2-4 (up to 4-8)** seconds of the last one accumulates 1 charge in the relic's internal buffer. The player's damage increases by **2.5-7.5 (up to 5-15)**% per charge, but they also take **5-2.5 (up to 7.5-3.8)**% more damage. If the player fails to attack within the given time to gain another charge, the relic's internal buffer is completely emptied, nullifying all accumulated bonuses.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incoming Damage | 5-2.5 | +5% of base per level | 7.5-3.8 |
| Dealt Damage | 2.5-7.5 | +10% of base per level | 5-15 |
| Duration | 2-4 | +10% of base per level | 4-8 |

### Phlebotomy

*Unlocks at relic level 5, passive. Ability max level 10.*

Increases the player's attack speed by **0.5-1 (up to 0.7-1.5)**% and movement speed by **1-2.5 (up to 2-5)**%, while also granting passive health regeneration of **0.002-0.005 (up to 0.004-0.01)**% per missing percent of the player's health.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heal | 0.002-0.005 | +10% of base per level | 0.004-0.01 |
| Movement Speed | 1-2.5 | +10% of base per level | 2-5 |
| Attack Speed | 0.5-1 | +5% of base per level | 0.7-1.5 |

### Spurt

*Unlocks at relic level 10, active (instantaneous). Ability max level 10.*

When activated, performs a dash in the direction of the player's gaze up to **3-8 (up to 12-32)** blocks, igniting, inflicting bleeding, and attacking every target in its path with the item in hand. Deals additional damage to each target equal to **0.1-0.25 (up to 0.2-0.5)** per charge stored in the relic's internal buffer from the «Berserker's Rage» ability and then empties it, before going on cooldown for **20-15 (up to 9.2-6.9)** seconds.

| Stat | Starting value | Per level | At max level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage | 0.1-0.25 | +10% of base per level | 0.2-0.5 |
| Distance | 3-8 | +30% of base per level | 12-32 |
| Cooldown | 20-15 | x0.925 per level | 9.2-6.9 |
