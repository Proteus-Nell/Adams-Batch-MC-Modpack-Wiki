## Relic abilities

Relic levelling: up to level **10**, first level costs **100** XP, +100 XP per level.

**How it earns experience:**

+1 experience point for every 5 units of size when hit by a snowball from the ability «Mold».

Found in loot chests of kind: chests in snowy biomes, chests in taiga biomes, chests in mountain biomes.

### Mold

*Passive. Ability max level 10.*

Collecting snow with an empty hand using RMB gradually forms special hardened snowballs, up to a size of **12-32 (up to 36-96)** units. Hitting a target with such a snowball deals **0.05-0.25 (up to 0.1-0.5)** damage, stuns for **0.025-0.05 (up to 0.062-0.125)** seconds, and freezes the target for **0.025-0.05 (up to 0.062-0.125)** per unit of snowball size. Holding a snowball in the inventory without the relic equipped also freezes the player.
