## What it does

A temporary purple platform made by the [Phantom Boot](../items/feet/phantom-boot.md). You can only stand on it while wearing Phantom Boots with the ability toggled on, and only if you aren't sneaking (sneak to drop through). Landing on it hard bounces you back up. It vanishes by itself after 4 to 6 seconds, and breaks instantly.
