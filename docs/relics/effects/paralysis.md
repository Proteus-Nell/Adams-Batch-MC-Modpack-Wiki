## What it does

You can't move, jump or sneak while it lasts. Mobs are frozen in place too. The [Jellyfish Necklace](../items/necklace/jellyfish-necklace.md) and the [Wonder of U](../../more-relics/items/back/wonder-of-u.md) inflict it.
