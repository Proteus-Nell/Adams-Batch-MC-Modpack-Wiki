## What it does

Makes you **completely invisible**, including your armor and held items, and mobs can't see you. The [Leafy Ring [WIP]](../items/ring/leafy-ring.md) and [Midnight Robe](../items/back/midnight-robe.md) give it for as long as their conditions hold.
