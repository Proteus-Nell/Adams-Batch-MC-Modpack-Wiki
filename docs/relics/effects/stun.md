## What it does

A stronger paralysis: you can't move, look around, attack or use items, and all your keys except Escape are ignored. Stars circle over the stunned creature's head. The [Infinite Ham](../items/relics/infinity-ham.md), thrown [Frozen snowball](../items/miscellaneous/solid-snowball.md)s and the [Wonder of U](../../more-relics/items/back/wonder-of-u.md) inflict it.
