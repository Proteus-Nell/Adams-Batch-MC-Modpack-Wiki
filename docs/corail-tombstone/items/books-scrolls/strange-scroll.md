## What it does

A blank scroll for making **Magic Scrolls** by combining it in your inventory (right-click it onto the ingredient):

| Combine with | Scroll of |
|---|---|
| Rabbit's Foot | Preservation |
| Ghast Tear | Unstable Intangibility |
| Feather | Feather Fall |
| Lapis Lazuli | Purification |
| Fermented Spider Eye | True Sight |
| Sunflower | Reach |
| Lightning Rod | Lightning Resistance |
| Snowball | Frost Resistance |
| Nautilus Shell | Aquatic Life |
| Honeycomb | Mercy |
| Glowstone Dust | [Scroll of Knowledge](../miscellaneous/scroll-of-knowledge.md) |

Used to make [Scroll of Aquatic Life](../miscellaneous/scroll-of-aquatic-life.md), [Scroll of Feather Falling](../miscellaneous/scroll-of-feather-fall.md), [Scroll of Frost Resistance](../miscellaneous/scroll-of-frost-resistance.md), [Scroll of Knowledge](../miscellaneous/scroll-of-knowledge.md), [Scroll of Lightning Resistance](../miscellaneous/scroll-of-lightning-resistance.md) and 6 more. It is crafted.
