## What it does

An old grave found in the world. Right-click it to **raid** it: you get loot (better with Luck) and some knowledge of death, and the grave crumbles. The first underwater grave you raid also gives the Coral Chant scroll, and until you've used a Ritual Flute there's a 1 in 5 chance of finding one. It can't be broken normally or blown up.
