> Your soul, your beats. Your shell is hard, and makes wonderful noises when it is banged against. Maybe it can help you.

## How it works

- Activated by pressing the skill key

## Obtaining

- Listed in the `intrinsicSkills` config option (config/mysticism/race/insect/ant_config.toml): The list of intrinsic skills that the race gets.

## Stats (config defaults)

Set in [`config/mysticism/ability/skill/intrinsic_config.toml`](../../configs/config-mysticism-ability-skill-intrinsic-config.md).

| Option | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `ClangorousSoul.tauntRadius` | 10 | The radius of the taunt. |
| `ClangorousSoul.tauntRadiusMastered` | 15 | The radius of the taunt when the skill is mastered. |
| `ClangorousSoul.cooldown` | 60 | The cooldown in seconds. |
| `ClangorousSoul.cooldownMastered` | 45 | The cooldown in seconds when the skill is mastered. |
