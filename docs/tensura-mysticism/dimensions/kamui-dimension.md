## What it does

The pocket world of the [Phaser](../abilities/unique-skills/phaser.md) skill, a dark, empty land where it's always noon.

**Getting in and out:** use Phaser's Authority of the Gods mode while holding **Shift** and keep it held for 3 s (half that once mastered; bigger bodies take longer). It costs 1,500 magicule and has a 5 s cooldown. Do it again inside Kamui to go back to the dimension you came from. Falling below **Y -60** in Kamui drops you into the Overworld.

Distances in Kamui are **10×** shorter than in the Overworld (the Nether is 8×), so it works as a shortcut: walk 100 blocks inside and you come out 1,000 blocks away.

Once Phaser is mastered, charging it without Shift while looking at a creature moves that creature. Inside Kamui it throws the creature out to the Overworld. Outside, the code is meant to pull the creature in, but in this version it moves **you** into Kamui at the creature's position instead.
