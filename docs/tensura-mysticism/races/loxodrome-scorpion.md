> An evolved scorpion, becoming the king of the pack. It uses deadly and lethal venom to destroy all of its targets from the inside.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Scorpion](scorpion.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Loxodrome Scorpion Insectar](loxodrome-scorpion-insectar.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Loxodrome Scorpion Insectar](loxodrome-scorpion-insectar.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Poison Soul Insect](poison-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Loxodrome Scorpion Insectar](loxodrome-scorpion-insectar.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Loxodrome Scorpion

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 10,000 | 100% |
