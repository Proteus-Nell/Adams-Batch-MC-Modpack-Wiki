> A Fire Ant that has gained a humanoid body. It delivers painful, stinging bites and fights ferociously.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Fire Ant](fire-ant.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Fire Ant Savant](fire-ant-savant.md), [Flame Soul Insect](flame-soul-insect.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Fire Ant Savant](fire-ant-savant.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Flame Soul Insect](flame-soul-insect.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Fire Ant Insectar

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 100,000 | 100% |
