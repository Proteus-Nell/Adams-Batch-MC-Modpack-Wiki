> A Stag Beetle that has gained a humanoid body. It can now funnel its strength into its fists, allowing it to dominate the battlefield.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Stag Beetle](stag-beetle.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Stag Beetle Savant](stag-beetle-savant.md), [Fantasy Soul Insect](fantasy-soul-insect.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Stag Beetle Savant](stag-beetle-savant.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Fantasy Soul Insect](fantasy-soul-insect.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Stag Beetle Insectar

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 100,000 | 100% |
