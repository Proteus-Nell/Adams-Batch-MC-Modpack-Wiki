> An evolved beetle, leaning into its offenses. Their tough horn allows them to jab opponents to deal great damage.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Beetle](beetle.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Stag Beetle Insectar](stag-beetle-insectar.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Stag Beetle Insectar](stag-beetle-insectar.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Fantasy Soul Insect](fantasy-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Stag Beetle Insectar](stag-beetle-insectar.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Stag Beetle

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 10,000 | 100% |
