> An evolved scorpion, with the capability of manipulating gravity itself. It floats around aimlessly and randomly stings things it finds interesting. It has never been hunted excessively due to its nature, so its defenses are extremely weak.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Scorpion](scorpion.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Singularity Scorpion Insectar](singularity-scorpion-insectar.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Singularity Scorpion Insectar](singularity-scorpion-insectar.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Gravity Soul Insect](gravity-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Singularity Scorpion Insectar](singularity-scorpion-insectar.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Singularity Scorpion

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 10,000 | 100% |
