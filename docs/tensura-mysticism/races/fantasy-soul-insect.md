> A Stag Beetle that has evolved "the right way" and became a spiritual life-form. It embodies the element of Fantasy perfectly, distorting magic and removing threats with pure might alone.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Stag Beetle Insectar](stag-beetle-insectar.md), [Stag Beetle](stag-beetle.md), [Drone Beetle](drone-beetle.md), [Drone Beetle Insectar](drone-beetle-insectar.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Divine Stag Beetle](divine-stag-beetle.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Divine Stag Beetle](divine-stag-beetle.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Divine Stag Beetle](divine-stag-beetle.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Fantasy Soul Insect

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 400,000 | 50% |
| Acquire name | 50% |
