> An evolved ant, leaning into its defenses. They naturally have good bulk, making them extremely tough to take out of a fight.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Ant](ant.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Hardshell Ant Insectar](hardshell-ant-insectar.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Hardshell Ant Insectar](hardshell-ant-insectar.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Earth Soul Insect](earth-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Hardshell Ant Insectar](hardshell-ant-insectar.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Hardshell Ant

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 10,000 | 100% |
