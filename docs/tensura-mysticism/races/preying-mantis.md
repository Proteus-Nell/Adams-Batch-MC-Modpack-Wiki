> An evolved mantis, leaning into its attack speed. It punches quick and deals decisive blows that almost guarantees victory.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Mantis](mantis.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Preying Mantis Insectar](preying-mantis-insectar.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Preying Mantis Insectar](preying-mantis-insectar.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Steel Soul Insect](steel-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Preying Mantis Insectar](preying-mantis-insectar.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Preying Mantis

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 10,000 | 100% |
