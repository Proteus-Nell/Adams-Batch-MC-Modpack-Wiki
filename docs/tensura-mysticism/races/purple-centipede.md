> An evolved centipede, leaning into its offenses. It creates spatial distortions to disorient its prey before striking.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Purple Centipede Insectar](purple-centipede-insectar.md), [Centipede](centipede.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Purple Centipede Insectar](purple-centipede-insectar.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Purple Centipede Insectar](purple-centipede-insectar.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Spatial Soul Insect](spatial-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Purple Centipede Insectar](purple-centipede-insectar.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Purple Centipede

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 10,000 | 100% |
