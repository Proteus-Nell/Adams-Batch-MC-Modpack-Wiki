> An evolved centipede, leaning into its speed. It swiftly dodges its target's attacks before paralyzing them, finishing the job.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Insect](insect.md), [Centipede](centipede.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Yellow Centipede Insectar](yellow-centipede-insectar.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Yellow Centipede Insectar](yellow-centipede-insectar.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Paralysis Soul Insect](paralysis-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Yellow Centipede Insectar](yellow-centipede-insectar.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Yellow Centipede

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 10,000 | 100% |
