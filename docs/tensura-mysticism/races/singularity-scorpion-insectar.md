## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Singularity Scorpion](singularity-scorpion.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Singularity Scorpion Savant](singularity-scorpion-savant.md), [Gravity Soul Insect](gravity-soul-insect.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Singularity Scorpion Savant](singularity-scorpion-savant.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Gravity Soul Insect](gravity-soul-insect.md)
