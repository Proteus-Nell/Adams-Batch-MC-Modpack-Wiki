> A Fire Ant that has evolved "the right way" and became a spiritual life-form. It carries out the will of the army, being a shining beacon for other Fire Ants.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Fire Ant Insectar](fire-ant-insectar.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Divine Fire Ant](divine-fire-ant.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Divine Fire Ant](divine-fire-ant.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Divine Fire Ant](divine-fire-ant.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Fire Ant Savant

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 400,000 | 50% |
| Kill 4 bosses | 50% |
