## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Singularity Scorpion Insectar](singularity-scorpion-insectar.md), [Singularity Scorpion](singularity-scorpion.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Gravity Soul Insect

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
