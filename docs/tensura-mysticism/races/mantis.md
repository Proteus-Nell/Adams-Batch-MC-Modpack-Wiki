> Mantises are fast-hitting insects that whittle down their opponents with quick strikes and their powerful melee skills. Some variants have even been known to melt through armor.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Insect](insect.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Preying Mantis](preying-mantis.md), [Lixivant Mantis](lixivant-mantis.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Preying Mantis](preying-mantis.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Steel Soul Insect](steel-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Preying Mantis](preying-mantis.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Mantis

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 3,000 | 100% |
