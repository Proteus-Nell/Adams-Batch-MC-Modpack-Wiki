> A Hardshell Ant that has gained a humanoid body. Its shell has only become tougher, and it will continue to become stronger.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Hardshell Ant](hardshell-ant.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Hardshell Ant Savant](hardshell-ant-savant.md), [Earth Soul Insect](earth-soul-insect.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Hardshell Ant Savant](hardshell-ant-savant.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Earth Soul Insect](earth-soul-insect.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Hardshell Ant Insectar

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 100,000 | 100% |
