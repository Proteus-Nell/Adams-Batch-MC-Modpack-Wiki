> An evolved centipede, leaning into its swimming ability. It loves to hunt in the vast oceans, making use of its ability to paralyze targets.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Centipede](centipede.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Blue Centipede Insectar](blue-centipede-insectar.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Blue Centipede Insectar](blue-centipede-insectar.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Water Soul Insect](water-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Blue Centipede Insectar](blue-centipede-insectar.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Blue Centipede

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 10,000 | 100% |
