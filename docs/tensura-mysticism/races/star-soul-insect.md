## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Queen Wasp Insectar](queen-wasp-insectar.md), [Queen Wasp](queen-wasp.md), [Army Wasp](army-wasp.md), [Army Wasp Insectar](army-wasp-insectar.md)

- **Default evolution:** [Divine Queen Wasp](divine-queen-wasp.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Divine Queen Wasp](divine-queen-wasp.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Star Soul Insect

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 400,000 | 50% |
| Acquire [Magic Jamming](../../tensura-reincarnated/abilities/extra-skills/magic-jamming.md) | 50% |
