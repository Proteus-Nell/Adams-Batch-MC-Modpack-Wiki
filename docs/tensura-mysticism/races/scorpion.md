> Scorpions deliver strikes with their powerful stinger, which can inflict a potent venom on their targets. In rare cases, some even gain the ability to manipulate Gravity itself.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Insect](insect.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Loxodrome Scorpion](loxodrome-scorpion.md), [Singularity Scorpion](singularity-scorpion.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Loxodrome Scorpion](loxodrome-scorpion.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Poison Soul Insect](poison-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Loxodrome Scorpion](loxodrome-scorpion.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Scorpion

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 3,000 | 100% |
