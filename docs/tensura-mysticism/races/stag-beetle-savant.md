> A Stag Beetle that has evolved "the right way" and became a spiritual life-form. Its exoskeleton hardens evermore, capable of utilising Mithril Strength to further enhance its capabilities.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Stag Beetle Insectar](stag-beetle-insectar.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Divine Stag Beetle](divine-stag-beetle.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Divine Stag Beetle](divine-stag-beetle.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Divine Stag Beetle](divine-stag-beetle.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Stag Beetle Savant

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 400,000 | 50% |
| Kill 4 bosses | 50% |
