> An evolved mantis, gaining the ability to corrode things. It melts through enemy defenses and cripples them before they even know what hit them.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Mantis](mantis.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Lixivant Mantis Insectar](lixivant-mantis-insectar.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Lixivant Mantis Insectar](lixivant-mantis-insectar.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Corrosion Soul Insect](corrosion-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Lixivant Mantis Insectar](lixivant-mantis-insectar.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Lixivant Mantis

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 10,000 | 100% |
