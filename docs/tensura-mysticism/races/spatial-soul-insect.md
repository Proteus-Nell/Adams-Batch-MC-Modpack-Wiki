## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Purple Centipede Insectar](purple-centipede-insectar.md), [Purple Centipede](purple-centipede.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Divine Purple Centipede](divine-purple-centipede.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Divine Purple Centipede](divine-purple-centipede.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Divine Purple Centipede](divine-purple-centipede.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Spatial Soul Insect

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 400,000 | 50% |
| Acquire [Spatial Manipulation](../../tensura-reincarnated/abilities/extra-skills/spatial-manipulation.md) | 50% |
