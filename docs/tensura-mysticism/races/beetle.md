> Beetles are heavy-hitting insects, focusing everything into their power. Though they are one of the stronger races, their defences are lacklustre, to put it nicely.

## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Insect](insect.md)
- **Evolves into:** [Stag Beetle](stag-beetle.md), [Drone Beetle](drone-beetle.md)
- **Default evolution:** [Stag Beetle](stag-beetle.md)
- **On awakening (True Demon Lord / True Hero):** [Lightning Soul Insect](lightning-soul-insect.md)
- **During the Harvest Festival:** [Stag Beetle](stag-beetle.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Beetle

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 3,000 | 100% |
