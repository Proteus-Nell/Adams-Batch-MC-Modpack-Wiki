## What it does

The scythe of the [Spiritualist](../../abilities/unique-skills/spiritualist.md) skill, which summons it into your hand. It deals no normal damage: a fully charged swing hurts the target's **spiritual health** directly for 30 up to 1,500 (scaling with your soul points, maxing at 50,000,000), and a weaker swing does a tenth of that.

A High Magisteel scythe you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **30** attack damage at **1** attack speed; one-handed **20** damage at **0.8** speed. It also has +2 blocks of reach, +20% critical hit chance and 75% sweeping damage. Durability: **2,500**.
