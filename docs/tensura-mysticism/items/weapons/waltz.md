## What it does

**Axiom** and **Waltz** are a matched pair of Hihi'irokane long swords (30,000 durability) carried by [Memoires](../../mobs/memoires.md). Waltz's hits give the target [Chill](../../../tensura-reincarnated/effects/chill.md) III for 10 seconds. The [Repeater](../../abilities/unique-skills/repeater.md) skill has a storehouse that holds the pair: use it with Axiom in your main hand and Waltz in your off hand to put them away, and again to draw both at once.

A Hihiirokane weapon you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **82** attack damage at **1.4** attack speed; one-handed **81** damage at **1.2** speed. It also has +1 block of reach and 25% sweeping damage. Durability: **3,600**.
