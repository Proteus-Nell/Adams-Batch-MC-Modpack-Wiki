## What it does

Constant's energy mode, which lasts 10 s. While it's active, **your skills cost nothing up front** and your magicule and aura don't regenerate. When it ends, everything you spent is taken at once. If you can't afford the bill, your magicule or aura is left at 25% of the maximum and you get [Constant: Energy Debuff](constant-energy-debuff.md).
