## What it does

Phaser's intangibility. Attacks and projectiles pass straight through you while it lasts (5 s). Phaser triggers it by itself when you're hit (if it's off cooldown), or you can activate it. It then goes on a 15 s cooldown. Void damage and creative players still get through.
