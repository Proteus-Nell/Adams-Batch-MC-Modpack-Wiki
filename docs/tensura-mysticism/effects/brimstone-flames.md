## What it does

Hellish fire. Every second it deals **15** flame damage per level, mostly elemental, which can't be dodged and ignores resistances. It also cripples healing: Aura Healing stops working, and Instant Regeneration only heals every 5 seconds, at 20% strength. Weapons with the [Holy Flames](../enchantments/holy-flames.md) enchantment inflict it for 5 seconds.
