## What it does

Tenacity's regeneration, paid with aura instead of magicule. Every half second it heals **all** your missing health, costing **100** aura per health point (60 once Tenacity is mastered). If you can't pay in full, it heals what you can afford and switches Tenacity off.

At **level II** (mastered Tenacity) it also restores spiritual health (120 aura per point, 80 mastered) and heals through Curse, Frost, Black Burn and [Brimstone Flames](brimstone-flames.md). At level I those block it.
