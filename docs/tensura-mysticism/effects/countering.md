## What it does

Restricted's counter stance. You can't move or attack while waiting. The **next physical hit** against you is completely blocked: the stance ends and the attacker becomes [Imbalanced](imbalanced.md) for 5 s. Press Restricted again to drop the stance without the cooldown.
