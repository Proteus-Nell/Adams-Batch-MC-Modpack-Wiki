## What it does

Jinchuriki's chakra cloak. Its bonuses grow with level:

| Level | Attack damage | Armor | Movement speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | +9 | +5 | +0.04 |
| II | +15 | +10 | +0.08 |
| III | +30 | +20 | +0.12 |
| IV | +36 | +35 | +0.15 |
| V+ | +45 | +60 | +0.18 |

It drains Jinchuriki's energy every second and ends when that runs out.
