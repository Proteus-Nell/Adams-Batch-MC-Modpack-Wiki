## What it does

Creeping frost. You're slowed by **30%**, plus 15% for each level above I (up to 95%). While frozen, battlewill and magic skills have a **50% chance to fail** when you use them (and go on a 5 second cooldown). It thaws over time, losing a level every 2 seconds. Weapons with the [Boreal Frost](../enchantments/boreal-frost.md) enchantment inflict it at level VI.
