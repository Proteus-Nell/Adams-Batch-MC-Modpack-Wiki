## What it does

Constant's destruction mode. While it lasts, **every hit you land deals a fixed amount of damage**: the highest single hit Constant has recorded for you (it tracks your biggest hit while its Physical mode is on), up to 5,000. Hold the skill to keep it up, for up to 10 s.
