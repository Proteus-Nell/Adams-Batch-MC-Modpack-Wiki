## What it does

Reducer's Emancipation: a holy coating. It gives +1 physical resistance degradation, and each melee hit adds a second **holy** hit worth **1.75×** your attack damage. It costs 300 energy every second and ends when you can't pay. Press Reducer again to turn it off.
