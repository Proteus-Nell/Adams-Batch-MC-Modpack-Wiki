## What it does

The **Paths**, a world of eternal midnight guarded by [Feldway](../mobs/feldway.md).

**Getting there:** find a [Paths Gate](../structures/paths-gate.md) (it generates in plains and sunflower plains) and stand in its portal for **5 seconds**. You need at least **5,000,000 max EP**, or the portal refuses you. Arriving builds a return portal at least at Y 110 above the spot you came from. Standing in it for 5 seconds takes you back to the gate you entered by.

**Summoning Feldway:** build a 3 × 3 floor of [Block of Pure Magisteel](../../tensura-reincarnated/blocks/pure-magisteel-block.md), put a [Block of Mithril](../../tensura-reincarnated/blocks/mithril-block.md) on the center with two blocks of air above it, and right-click the Mithril Block. It's used up and Feldway appears (only one at a time within 96 blocks). This only works in the Paths.
