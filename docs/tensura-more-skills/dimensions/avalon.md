## What it does

The **Kingdom of Avalon**, a sunlit dueling ground made by [Avalon, God of the Eternal Kingdom](../abilities/ultimate-skills/avalon-god-of-eternal-kingdom.md).

**How you get here:** use the Kingdom of Avalon mode while looking at a living enemy within 48 blocks. It costs 50,000 magicule and has a 120 s cooldown. You and the target are pulled into an arena of your own, facing each other 22 blocks apart.

**Inside:**

- Neither of you can leave the arena (about 62 blocks across from the center); stepping out puts you back in.
- Every half second, everyone within 70 blocks who isn't the user or their ally gets [Silence](../../tensura-reincarnated/effects/silence.md), mobs also get [Anti-Skill](../../tensura-reincarnated/effects/anti-skill.md), and toggled skills are switched off every second.
- Avalon's Royal Laws can only be declared here.

It ends after 120 s, when either fighter dies or leaves, or when the user uses the mode again while sneaking. Both are then sent back to where they were.
