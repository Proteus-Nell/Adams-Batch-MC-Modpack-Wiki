## What it does

**Feldway**, the leader of the angels and the boss of the [Paths](../dimensions/paths.md). He's summoned at a shrine in the Paths: a 3 × 3 floor of Pure Magisteel Blocks with a Mithril Block on the center (right-click the Mithril Block).

The fight has three phases. Feldway calls angels to his side, dashes, and brings down Sky Judgement, Lance Rain, Solar Prison and Heavenfall, which hit much harder in phase three (Sky Judgement up to 1,800 damage). Partway through he releases [Michael Manas](michael-manas.md). Once Michael is dead and Feldway is brought down, he doesn't die: he **awakens** into a second boss ([Feldway Awakening](feldway-awakening.md), then [Feldway Awakened](feldway-awakened.md)).

A boss with **12,000** health and **220** attack damage.
