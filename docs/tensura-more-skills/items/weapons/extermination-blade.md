## What it does

The **Extermination Blade** summoned by [Adaptability](../../abilities/unique-skills/adaptability.md) (and Divine General Mahoraga). It comes with Severance, Tsukumogami, Unbreaking and Sharpness, and it can't be dropped (if it ends up on the ground anyway it can't be destroyed).

It holds up to **50,000** EP. Each hit spends 180 EP (240 once you've adapted to severance) to deal a second, bonus hit of 0.75 + up to 2 from the blade's EP + 12% of your attack damage, capped at **6**.

A Pure Magisteel weapon you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **35** attack damage at **1.4** attack speed; one-handed **33** damage at **1.2** speed. It also has +1 block of reach and no sweeping attack. Durability: **3,000**.
