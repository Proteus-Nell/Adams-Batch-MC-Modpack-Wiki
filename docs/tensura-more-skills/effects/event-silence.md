## What it does

Events around you are silenced. Per level: **-55%** movement speed, **-95%** attack and chant speed and **-65%** magicule and aura regeneration. Istaroth's ultimate techniques inflict it.

| Attack Speed | -0.95 | multiply total |
| Chant Speed | -0.95 | multiply total |
| Magicule Regeneration Multiplier | -0.65 | multiply total |
| Aura Regeneration Multiplier | -0.65 | multiply total |
