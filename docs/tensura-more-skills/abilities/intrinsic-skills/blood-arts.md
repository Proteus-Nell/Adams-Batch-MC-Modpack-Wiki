> An intrinsic Bloodfiend art that manipulates stolen Blood. Drain blood from enemies, awaken Blood Surge to amplify damage, guard yourself with Crimson Guard, brand prey, or perform the Kindred Rite on weakened mobs.

## Modes

| # | Mode |
|---|---|
| 1 | Drain Blood |
| 2 | Crimson Guard |
| 3 | Blood Brand |
| 4 | Kindred Rite |

## Costs

| When | Magicule (MP) | Aura (AP) |
|---|---|---|
| Always | 0 |  |

## How it works

- Activated by pressing the skill key
- Charged or channelled by holding the skill key

- Intrinsic skill of: [Bloodfiend](../../races/bloodfiend.md), [Elder Bloodfiend](../../races/elder-bloodfiend.md), [Blood Noble](../../races/blood-noble.md), [Blood Monarch](../../races/blood-monarch.md), [Crimson Progenitor](../../races/crimson-progenitor.md)
