## What it does

A door or trapdoor linked to someone's [Domicile](../dimensions/domicile.md), made when they use the [Domicile](../abilities/unique-skills/domicile.md) skill on an ordinary door or trapdoor. **Walk through it while it's open** to enter the owner's Homestead or Shop (anyone can, with a 2-second cooldown between trips). Opening the door from inside takes you back out through it. Linking a new door turns the old one back into a plain oak door, and the linked doors can't be broken from inside.

Decorative trapdoor building block.
