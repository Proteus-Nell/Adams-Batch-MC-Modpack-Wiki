## What it does

Unbreakable ice crystal made by [Cessation](../abilities/unique-skills/cessation.md) and [｢ Gabriel, Lord of Patience ｣](../abilities/ultimate-skills/gabriel.md). While you hold their ice abilities it forms either a hollow **shell** around you that moves with you, or an **ice wall** in front of you (5 wide, 8 high and 3 thick with Cessation). It only fills empty space and disappears as soon as you let go.
