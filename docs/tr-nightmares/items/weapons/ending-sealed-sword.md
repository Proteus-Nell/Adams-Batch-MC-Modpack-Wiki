## What it does

The sealed form of the [Ending](../../abilities/unique-skills/ending.md) skill's sword; the skill creates it. If you have Ending, every hit gives the target [Core Damage](../../effects/core-damage.md) II for 5 seconds. If you don't, trying to use it destroys it. Repair it with Daemon Essence.

A Pure Magisteel sword you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **36** attack damage at **1.4** attack speed; one-handed **35** damage at **1.2** speed. It also has +1 block of reach and 25% sweeping damage. Durability: **3,000**.
