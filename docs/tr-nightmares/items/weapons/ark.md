## What it does

One of the divine-tier **Genesis** swords. It has no ability of its own: it's a stat weapon with long reach, a strong sweep and a 20% critical chance (see the stats box), and 10,000 durability.

A Divine Tools sword you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **110** attack damage at **1.5** attack speed; one-handed **110** damage at **1.5** speed. It also has +2 blocks of reach, +20% critical hit chance and 75% sweeping damage. Durability: **10,000**. It is made at the Tensura Smithing.
