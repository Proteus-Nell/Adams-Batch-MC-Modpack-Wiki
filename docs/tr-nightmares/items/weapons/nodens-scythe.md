## What it does

A Hihiirokane scythe you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **257** attack damage at **7** attack speed; one-handed **256** damage at **6.8** speed. It also has +2 blocks of reach, +50% critical hit chance and 75% sweeping damage. Durability: **3,600**.
