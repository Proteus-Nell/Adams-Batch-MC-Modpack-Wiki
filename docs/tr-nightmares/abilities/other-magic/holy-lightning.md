> A holy-element spell that strikes enemies with sanctified lightning.

## Costs

| When | Magicule (MP) | Aura (AP) |
|---|---|---|
| Always | 15,000 |  |

## How it works

- Activated by pressing the skill key
- Charged or channelled by holding the skill key
- Triggers when the held key is released
- Triggers when you damage a target

## Obtaining

- Intrinsic skill of: [Lesser Holy Dragon](../../races/lesser-holy-dragon.md), [Medium Holy Dragon](../../races/medium-holy-dragon.md), [Saint Dragon](../../races/saint-dragon.md), [Heavenly Dragon](../../races/heavenly-dragon.md), [Divine Heavenly Dragon](../../races/divine-heavenly-dragon.md)
- Can be found in skill tomes
- Can appear in rare tomes in buried wizard towers
- Can appear in rare tomes in rotted wizard towers
- Can appear in rare tomes in ruined wizard towers
- Can appear in uncommon tomes in burnt wizard towers
