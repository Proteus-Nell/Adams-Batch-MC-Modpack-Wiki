> A unique skill of overwhelming water control. Wet Like Water grants Mirage, Water, and Ice Aspectual Magics. Mist sets a substitute body, Rainfall summons explosive water illusions, and Slip lets you surge across water surfaces.

## Modes

| # | Mode |
|---|---|
| 1 | Mist |
| 2 | Rainfall |
| 3 | Slip |

## How it works

- Activated by pressing the skill key
- Charged or channelled by holding the skill key
- Has a continuous (per-tick) effect
- Triggers when you damage a target
- Triggers when you die
- Does something when first learned
