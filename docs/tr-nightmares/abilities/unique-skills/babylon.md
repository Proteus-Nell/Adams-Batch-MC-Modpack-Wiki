> Golden Rule in your slot. Enkidu power rises against divine, awakened, and higher-EP foes. King's Storehouse (2×5 weapons) feeds the Gate of Babylon, with Surround Attack unlocked at mastery.

## How it works

- Activated by pressing the skill key
- Charged or channelled by holding the skill key
- Triggers when the held key is released
- Adjusted by scrolling while active
- Has a continuous (per-tick) effect
