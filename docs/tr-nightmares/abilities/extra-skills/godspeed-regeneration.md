> Toggle on to convert MP into instant HP and SHP recovery. HP is handled before instant regeneration effects; SHP yields to them.

## Modes

| # | Mode |
|---|---|
| 1 | Godspeed |

## How it works

- Can be toggled on and off
- Has a continuous (per-tick) effect
- Triggers when you take damage
