> Soul dominion over death and transference. In-slot attacks gain spiritual bonus from souls. Toggle Soul Shield to consume souls on fatal damage. Hold Soul Transmogrification to preserve Skill Storage memories through reset. Soul Storage revives dead player subordinates in hardcore.

## Modes

| # | Mode |
|---|---|
| 1 | Soul Transmogrification |
| 2 | Soul Storage |
| 3 | Skill Transmogrification |
| 4 | Soul Mimicry |

## How it works

- Activated by pressing the skill key
- Charged or channelled by holding the skill key
- Triggers when the held key is released
- Adjusted by scrolling while active
- Has a continuous (per-tick) effect
- Triggers when you damage a target
- Triggers on melee contact
- Triggers when you are attacked
- Triggers when you take damage
- Triggers when an effect is applied to you
- Triggers when a projectile hits you
- Triggers when you die
- Triggers when you respawn
