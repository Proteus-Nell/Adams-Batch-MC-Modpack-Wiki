## What it does

**Masayuki, the Lightspeed Chosen Hero**, a sentient boss wielding a Hihi'irokane long sword. Killing him while you have the Gift or Astraea skill teaches you Hero Banner Blessing.

**Spawning:** he isn't in the natural spawn rules in this version.

**Stats:** 800 health, 4,500 spiritual health, 80 armor, 15 attack damage, 400,000 EP.

**Rewards:** killing it raises your max magicule and aura by about **10% of 10,000,000** (scaled by your alignment and the EP gain gamerule); it drops a 50% chance of [Holy Essence](../items/materials/holy-essence.md) (2 to 10); it can teach Absolute Severance (the chance is set by `otherworlderSkillDrop`, which is **0** in this pack, so it doesn't happen here).

It takes at most **500** damage from any one hit.

A boss with **5,000,000** magicule.
