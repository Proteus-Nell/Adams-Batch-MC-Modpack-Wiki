## What it does

A huge transformation, only obtainable through commands in this version (no skill applies it). Its bonuses are multiplied by (level + 1): **+20,000** max health, **+450** attack damage, **+300** armor, **+0.1** movement speed and **+0.5** knockback resistance at each step.

It also triples your max magicule and aura, refills both, and lets you fly. When it ends, you get the transformation hangover: [Weakness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Weakness) II, [Fragility](../../tensura-reincarnated/effects/fragility.md) II and [Paralysis](../../tensura-reincarnated/effects/paralysis.md) I for 10 minutes.
