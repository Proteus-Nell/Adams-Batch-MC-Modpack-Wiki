## What it does

Stops all healing over time. Every second it removes Regeneration, Instant Regeneration, Self Regeneration, [Slowheal](slowheal.md), [Glorious Regen](glorious-regen.md) and [Divine Regeneration](divine-regeneration.md), and it shuts off your magicule regeneration while it lasts.
