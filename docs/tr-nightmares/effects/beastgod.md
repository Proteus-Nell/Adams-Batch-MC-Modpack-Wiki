## What it does

Beast God, the Nemean Lord and Eyaluth transformation. Every stat bonus is multiplied by (level + 1):

| Bonus | Level I | Level II |
|---|---|---|
| Max health | +800 | +1,200 |
| Attack damage | +50 | +75 |
| Armor | +24 | +36 |
| Movement speed | +0.2 | +0.3 |
| Knockback resistance | +1.0 | +1.5 |

It also **triples** your max magicule and aura, refills both when it starts, and lets you fly. While it lasts you regain **0.2%** of your max magicule and aura and **1** health every second. Nemean Lord's Beast Rampage pushes it to level 10 or higher.

When it ends, you lose the flight and get the transformation hangover: [Weakness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Weakness) II, [Fragility](../../tensura-reincarnated/effects/fragility.md) II and [Paralysis](../../tensura-reincarnated/effects/paralysis.md) I for 10 minutes. Turning Nemean Lord off early instead gives Slowness III, Weakness III and [Magic Interference](../../tensura-reincarnated/effects/magic-interference.md) II for 15 minutes.
