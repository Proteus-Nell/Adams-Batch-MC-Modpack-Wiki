## What it does

Raziel's ink. Each physical hit from Raziel adds one level, up to 30, and the effect lasts 30 seconds.
- At level 5 the target is temporarily renamed.
- At level 12 it gets Anti-Skill for 20 seconds.
- At level 30 Raziel can **erase** it.

## Applied by

[Raziel, Lord of Secrets](../abilities/ultimate-skills/raziel.md)
