## What it does

A paradox flame that burns cold. It gives **-50%** movement speed per level and keeps you covered in [Frost](../../tensura-reincarnated/effects/frost.md). It also puts out and blocks normal fire. Every second it deals **10** ice damage plus **10** flame-breath damage, and this damage can't be dodged.

Freezing Flame's white-flame hits inflict it for 10 seconds (level II when mastered), and so do Paradox flames. Creatures with Spiritual Attack Nullification or Thermal Fluctuation Nullification toggled on are immune.
