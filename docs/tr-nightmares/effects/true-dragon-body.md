## What it does

Pseudo Dragon Body's true-dragon form. While it's active, your base max health becomes **1,400**, attack **21**, armor **75** and max spiritual health **8,560**. You're fully healed and your max EP is **quadrupled**.

It drains **500** magicule every tick. When your magicule runs out or you turn the skill off, your old stats come back. It can't be used together with Tensura's Dragon Mode.
