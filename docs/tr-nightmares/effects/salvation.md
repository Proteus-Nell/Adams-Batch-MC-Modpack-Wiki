## What it does

Azrael's mark. Each physical hit from Azrael has a **0.3** chance (0.6 mastered) to add 2 levels, up to 8 (12 mastered). The mark lasts 30 seconds.

Azrael's Retribution and Purgatory attacks go after marked targets: Retribution deals 250 damage per level, and each Purgatory slash removes some levels.

## Applied by
