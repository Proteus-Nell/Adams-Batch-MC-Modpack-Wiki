## What it does

Soul Shrine's and Izanagi's "Zone", entered through Flash strikes. While the skill is toggled on, each physical hit has a small chance (**1%**, or 5% mastered) to become a **Flash**. A Flash deals **2.5×** damage and puts you in the Zone for 50 seconds.

While you're in the Zone, Flash chance goes up by 20%. At levels II to IV the Zone gives **+120%** base attack damage and attack speed; from level V up only **+20%**, because of how the formula rounds.
