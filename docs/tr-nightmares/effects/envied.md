## What it does

Leviathan's and Cthulhu's "Crushing Jealousy" mark. It lands on a creature that has more magicule or aura than the attacker. While it's marked, the attacker's hits drain a share of its magicule or aura (picked at random) into the attacker. If the attacker has more energy instead, the target gets [Stolen Luck](stolen-luck.md).
