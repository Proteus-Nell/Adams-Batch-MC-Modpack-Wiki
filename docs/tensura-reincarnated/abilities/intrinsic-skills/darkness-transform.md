> Channel your inner darkness to burn and blind nearby opponents.

## How it works

- Triggers when you damage a target

## Obtaining

- Can be learned by: [Corrupted Dragonkin](../../../ascension/races/corrupted-dragonkin.md), [Corrupted Dragon](../../../ascension/races/corrupted-dragon.md), [Cursed Dragon](../../../ascension/races/cursed-dragon.md), [Demonic Dragon](../../../ascension/races/demonic-dragon.md), [Demon Dragon God](../../../ascension/races/demon-dragon-god.md)
- Innate to mobs: [Shadow Imp](../../../tensura-mysticism/mobs/shadow-imp.md)
