> Become able to cast mastered magic instantly, in water or even under the effects of silence.

## Obtaining

- Listed in the `intrinsicSkills` config option (config/nightmare/race/scholar_config.toml): List of skills obtained by this race.
- Listed in the `grantedSkillIds` config option (config/tensuramoreskills-grand.toml): Skill IDs granted by Elfaria. Includes Albis so old Ars Weiss clones stay compatible, magic basics, transforms, senses, chant annulment,...
