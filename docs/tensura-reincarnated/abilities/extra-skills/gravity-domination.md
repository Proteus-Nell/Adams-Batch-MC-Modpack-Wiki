> Boosts Gravity abilities by a large amount and allows you to fly without hindrance.

## Costs

| When | Magicule (MP) | Aura (AP) |
|---|---|---|
| Always | 1,000 |  |

## How it works

- Can be toggled on and off
- Activated by pressing the skill key
- Triggers when you damage a target

## Obtaining

- Acquisition checks: [Gravity Manipulation](gravity-manipulation.md)
