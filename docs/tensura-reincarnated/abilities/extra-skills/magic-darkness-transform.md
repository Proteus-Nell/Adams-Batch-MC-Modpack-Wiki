> Gain access to the intermediate Darkness Spirit Magic and boost your darkness attacks while buffing yourself and debuffing enemies.

## Obtaining

- Intrinsic skill of: [Hell Rizer](../../../tr-nightmares/races/hell-rizer.md), [Ten Commandment](../../../tr-nightmares/races/ten-commandments.md), [Demon Prince](../../../tr-nightmares/races/demon-prince.md), [Dark Fairy](../../../tr-nightmares/races/dark-fairy.md), [Corrupted Dragon](../../../ascension/races/corrupted-dragon.md), [Cursed Dragon](../../../ascension/races/cursed-dragon.md), [Demonic Dragon](../../../ascension/races/demonic-dragon.md), [Demon Dragon God](../../../ascension/races/demon-dragon-god.md)
- Can be learned by: [Venom Lord](../../../ascension/races/venom-lord.md), [Frog Monarch](../../../ascension/races/frog-monarch.md), [Sovereign Djinn](../../../ascension/races/sovereign-djinn.md), [Infinite Djinn](../../../ascension/races/infinite-djinn.md), [Omniversal Djinn](../../../ascension/races/omniversal-djinn.md), [Phantom Corsair](../../../ascension/races/phantom-corsair.md), [Davy Jones](../../../ascension/races/davy-jones.md), [Corrupted Dragonkin](../../../ascension/races/corrupted-dragonkin.md), [Lich](../../../ascension/races/lich.md), [Lich King](../../../ascension/races/lich-king.md)
- Listed in the `grantedSkillIds` config option (config/tensuramoreskills-grand.toml): Skill IDs granted by Elfaria. Includes Albis so old Ars Weiss clones stay compatible, magic basics, transforms, senses, chant annulment,...
