## What it does

A hostile mob with **50** health, **15** attack damage and **6,000-9,000** magicule. Spawns naturally in Aqua Frog Spawn. It has 5 skills you can take from it with Predator-type skills. Drops [Elemental Shard (Water)](../items/materials/water-elemental-shard.md) and [Elemental Essence](../items/materials/elemental-essence.md).
