## What it does

A hostile mob with **40** health, **8** attack damage and **4,000-4,500** magicule. Spawns naturally in Hell Moth Spawn. It has 1 skill you can take from it with Predator-type skills. Drops [Gehenna-Moth Silk](../items/miscellaneous/gehenna-moth-silk.md) and [Hell-Moth Silk](../items/miscellaneous/hell-moth-silk.md).
