## What it does

A hostile mob with **35** health, **6** attack damage and **3,600-4,300** magicule. Spawns naturally in Direwolf Spawn. It has 2 skills you can take from it with Predator-type skills. Drops [Monster Leather (C)](../items/miscellaneous/monster-leather-c.md).
