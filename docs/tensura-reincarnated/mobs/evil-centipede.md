## What it does

A hostile mob with **60** health, **10** attack damage and **6,000-7,000** magicule. Spawns naturally in Evil Centipede Spawn. It has 1 skill you can take from it with Predator-type skills. Drops [Centipede Stinger](../items/miscellaneous/centipede-stinger.md).
