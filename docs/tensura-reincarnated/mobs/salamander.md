## What it does

A hostile mob with **30** health, **6** attack damage and **7,000-8,500** magicule. Spawns naturally in Salamander Spawn. It has 4 skills you can take from it with Predator-type skills. Drops [Elemental Shard (Fire)](../items/materials/fire-elemental-shard.md) and [Elemental Essence](../items/materials/elemental-essence.md).
