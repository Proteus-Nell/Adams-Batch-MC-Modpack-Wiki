## What it does

An invisible wall in the [Labyrinth](../dimensions/labyrinth.md). It holds you back (and pushes you toward the [Elemental Colossus](../mobs/elemental-colossus.md)) until you've **passed the Colossus**; after that you can walk through. It always blocks projectiles (they bounce back) and the Labyrinth's spirit protectors, and some teleport skills can't land on it. It can't be broken; creative players can right-click to switch it off.
