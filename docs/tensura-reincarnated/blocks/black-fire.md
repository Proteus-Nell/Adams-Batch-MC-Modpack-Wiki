## What it does

Black flames, left by black flame attacks, black lightning, Amaterasu and Black Fire Charges. Anything standing in it takes 2 black flame damage, catches fire for 10 seconds and gets [Black Burn](../effects/black-burn.md) for 10 seconds. It burns out over time, except on top of magic ore and magic metal blocks, where it **burns forever** like fire on netherrack.
