## What it does

The spirits' altar in the [Labyrinth](../dimensions/labyrinth.md). **Sneak on it for 10 seconds** (you're blinded while praying) to ask the elemental spirits for a contract. You get a random element, and the spirit's rank depends on your race: by default 40% Lesser, 30% Medium, 20% Greater and 1% Spirit Lord (a Lord only if you already have at least a Medium spirit of that element), otherwise nothing. You can pray again after 1,200 seconds (20 minutes). It can't be broken.
