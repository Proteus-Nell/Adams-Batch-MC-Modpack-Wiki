## What it does

The way into the [Labyrinth](../dimensions/labyrinth.md), found in Labyrinth Trees. It takes you to the Labyrinth's entrance, or past the [Elemental Colossus](../mobs/elemental-colossus.md) if you've already beaten it. The portal inside takes you back to the Overworld (to your bed if it's there). It can't be broken.
