## What it does

The Engorger unique skill's growth form. Per level: **+10** attack damage, **+10** armor, **+1** attack knockback, **+0.4** knockback resistance, **+0.1** movement and swim speed, **+0.1** jump strength, **+2.5** reach and +1 step height. You also grow to **1.5×** size.

Every second it feeds you (players) or heals you (mobs). Engorger keeps it on while toggled.
