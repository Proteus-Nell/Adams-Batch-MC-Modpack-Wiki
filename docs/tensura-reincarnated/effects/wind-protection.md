## What it does

A wrapping of wind. Per level: **+20%** movement speed and **+100%** projectile dodge chance, so projectiles almost never hit you. From level II, each level above I also gives **50%** shorter burning, **+0.1** knockback resistance and **+0.1** flame boost. Wind Protection magic gives level 1 for 120 s, or level 3 for 180 s once mastered.
