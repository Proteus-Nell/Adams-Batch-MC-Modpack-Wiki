## What it does

A barrier against magic. It removes [Chill](chill.md) every second and gives +1 magic barrier. Against Tensura magic damage it works like this (percentages of your health when the barrier went up, mastered values in brackets):

- Hits smaller than **16.7%** (20%) of that health are reduced by **16.7%** (20%) of that health.
- Bigger hits are reduced by **8.3%** (10%) of that health.

An attacker strong enough to shatter barriers breaks it instantly. Magic Barrier magic gives it for 10 s (20 s mastered); Reinforced Barrier and greater and arch daemons also use it.
