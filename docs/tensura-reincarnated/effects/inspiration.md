## What it does

A commander's rallying buff. Per level: **+0.3×** base attack damage, attack speed, attack knockback, knockback resistance, max health, movement speed, jump strength and swim speed, and **+30%** critical hit chance. Inspired mobs also stay calm instead of going berserk from Rampage or Arrogance.

Commander gives it to allies within 30 blocks, and Captivator, Hidden Ruler, Ruler, Subjugator, naming evolutions and several addon skills give it too.
