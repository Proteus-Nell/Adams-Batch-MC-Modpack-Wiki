## What it does

Magicule overload. Every second it deals **2** damage per level and gives Nausea. You get it when your magicule goes more than **25%** over your maximum (a higher level the further over you are), for example after absorbing too much. Some foods and Chrono Leech also cause it. Many angelic and high-tier skills make you immune.
