## What it does

Beast Transformation (level I) and Royal Beast (level II). Per level: **+15** attack damage, **+10** armor and **+0.04** movement speed. It also **doubles** your max magicule and aura (not per level) and refills both when it starts. Royal Beast (level II) also lets you fly.

When it ends, your current magicule and aura are **halved**, and you get the transformation hangover: [Weakness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Weakness) II, [Fragility](fragility.md) II and [Paralysis](paralysis.md) I for 10 minutes. Only one transformation can be active at a time.
