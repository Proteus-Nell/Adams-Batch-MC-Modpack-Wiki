## What it does

The buff from Search Enemy magic (120 s, or kept up while its mastered "constant" mode is held). It adds **+0.1** to your presence sense radius, which is tiny next to the 30-block base, so on its own it barely changes what you can sense. While it lasts, Search Enemy gains mastery. Pressing the spell again ends it early.
