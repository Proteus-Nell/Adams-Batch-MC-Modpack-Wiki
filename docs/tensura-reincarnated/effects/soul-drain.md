## What it does

Your soul is being eaten. Every half second it deals **10** spiritual damage per level. Merciless's hits give level 1 for 5 s; Soul Manipulation, Soul Domination, Beelzebuth and Feldway's angels also inflict it. Angelic skills (Solomon, Camael, Sunshine Grace, Vehement) make you immune.
