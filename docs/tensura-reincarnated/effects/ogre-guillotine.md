## What it does

Ogre Sword Guillotine (battlewill). At level I: attack damage **×1.5**, attack reach **×1.5** and attack speed **×0.8**. Level II doubles each of those changes. Your physical hits count as battlewill damage while it lasts, which ki and chi releases need. The art gives level I for 15 s, or level II for 30 s once mastered. Press it again to end it.
