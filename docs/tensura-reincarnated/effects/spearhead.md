## What it does

The Spearhead skill's rally. Per level: **+10** attack damage, **+5** armor, **+0.02** movement speed and **+1** swim speed. Spearhead gives it to allies within 20 blocks (level II when mastered). Subordinates you've set as meat shields also move to the spot you're looking at.
