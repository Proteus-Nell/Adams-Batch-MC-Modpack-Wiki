## What it does

The luck buff from Tuner (also given by Mood Maker and Phoenix while they're active, and for 10 minutes by Yog-Sothoth's Courage). Per level: **+30** attack damage, **+0.2** attack speed, **+0.04** movement speed, **+1** swim speed, and **+15%** melee and **+15%** projectile dodge chance.
