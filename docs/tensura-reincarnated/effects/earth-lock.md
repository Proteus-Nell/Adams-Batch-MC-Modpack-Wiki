## What it does

Stone armor that roots you in place. Per level: **+0.2** knockback resistance and the same explosion knockback resistance. You're shown covered in stone while it lasts. Earth Lock magic gives it for 12 s, and Lesser Daemons give it to themselves when they fire stone shots.
