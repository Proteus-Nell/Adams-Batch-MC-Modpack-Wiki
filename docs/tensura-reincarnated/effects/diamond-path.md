## What it does

Diamond Path Art (battlewill). Per level: **+10** armor and **+0.4** knockback resistance. Pressing the art gives level I for 60 s, or level II for 180 s once mastered. It removes [Chill](chill.md) every second.

It also works like an elemental resistance: fire, water, wind, earth, space, gravity, lightning, light, dark, heat, cold and corrosion damage is **blocked**, unless the hit is at least 50% of your current health (then it goes through, reduced to that same percentage). Hits that bypass resistances, or come from an attacker with Resistance Degradation, get through normally.
