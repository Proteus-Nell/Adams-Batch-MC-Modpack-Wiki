You need it to forge [Diamond Sickle](../tools/diamond-sickle.md), [Diamond Great Sword](../weapons/diamond-great-sword.md), [Diamond Katana](../weapons/diamond-katana.md), [Diamond Kodachi](../weapons/diamond-kodachi.md), [Diamond Long Sword](../weapons/diamond-long-sword.md), [Diamond Odachi](../weapons/diamond-odachi.md) and 4 more.
