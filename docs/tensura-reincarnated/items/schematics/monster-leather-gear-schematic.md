You need it to forge [Saddle](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Saddle), [Book](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Book), [Diamond Horse Armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Diamond_Horse_Armor), [Golden Horse Armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Golden_Horse_Armor), [Iron Horse Armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Horse_Armor), [Leather Horse Armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leather_Horse_Armor) and 23 more.
