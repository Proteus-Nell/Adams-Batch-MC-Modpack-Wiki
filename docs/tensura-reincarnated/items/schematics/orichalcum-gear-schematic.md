## What it does

You need it to forge [Enchanted Golden Apple](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enchanted_Golden_Apple), [Skill Reset Scroll](../books-scrolls/skill-reset-scroll.md), [Orichalcum Axe](../tools/orichalcum-axe.md), [Orichalcum Boots](../armor/orichalcum-boots.md), [Orichalcum Chestplate](../armor/orichalcum-chestplate.md), [Orichalcum Great Sword](../weapons/orichalcum-great-sword.md) and 15 more.
