## What it does

A tachi you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **82** attack damage at **1.4** attack speed; one-handed **81** damage at **1.2** speed. It also has +1 block of reach, +20% critical hit chance and 25% sweeping damage. Durability: **3,600**. It is made at the Smithing Bench.
