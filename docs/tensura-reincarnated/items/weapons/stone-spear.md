## What it does

A spear you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **5** attack damage at **1.4** attack speed; one-handed **4** damage at **1** speed. It also has +2 blocks of reach and no sweeping attack. Durability: **131**. It is made at the Smithing Bench.
