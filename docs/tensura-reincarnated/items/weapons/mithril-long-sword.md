## What it does

A long sword you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **28** attack damage at **1.4** attack speed; one-handed **27** damage at **1.2** speed. It also has +1 block of reach and 25% sweeping damage. Durability: **2,700**. It is made at the Smithing Bench.
