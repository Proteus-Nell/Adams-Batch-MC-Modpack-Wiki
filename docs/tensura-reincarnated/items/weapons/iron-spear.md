## What it does

A spear you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **6** attack damage at **1.4** attack speed; one-handed **5** damage at **1** speed. It also has +2 blocks of reach and no sweeping attack. Durability: **250**. It is made at the Smithing Bench and found in 5 kinds of loot chest.
