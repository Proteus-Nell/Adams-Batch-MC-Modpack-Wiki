## What it does

A great sword you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **11** attack damage at **0.8** attack speed; one-handed **0** damage at **0** speed. It also has +2 blocks of reach and 50% sweeping damage. Durability: **2,031**. It is made at a smithing table.
