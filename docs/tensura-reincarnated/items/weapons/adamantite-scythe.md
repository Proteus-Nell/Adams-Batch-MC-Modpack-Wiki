## What it does

A scythe you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **52** attack damage at **0.8** attack speed; one-handed **51** damage at **0.6** speed. It also has +2 blocks of reach, +50% critical hit chance and 75% sweeping damage. Durability: **3,200**. It is made at the Smithing Bench.
