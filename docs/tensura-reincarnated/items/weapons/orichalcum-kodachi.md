## What it does

A kodachi that deals **28** attack damage at **2** attack speed. It also has -0.75 blocks of reach, +20% critical hit chance, +0.5 critical damage multiplier and no sweeping attack. Durability: **2,800**. It is made at the Smithing Bench.
