## What it does

A katana you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **5** attack damage at **1.8** attack speed; one-handed **4** damage at **1.6** speed. It also has +20% critical hit chance and 25% sweeping damage. Durability: **59**. It is made at the Smithing Bench.
