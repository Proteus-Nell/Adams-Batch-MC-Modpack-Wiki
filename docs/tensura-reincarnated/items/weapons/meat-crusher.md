## What it does

A heavy two-handed club (the Orc Lord's weapon). Hold right-click and release to **slam** the ground in front of you: everything along the line takes your attack damage, gets [Corrosion](../../effects/corrosion.md) II for 3 seconds and is knocked up, and blocks may be thrown around if skill griefing is on. The slam costs 10 durability and has a 2-second cooldown.

With the **Soul Eater** enchantment and [Starved](../../abilities/unique-skills/starved.md) toggled on, using it instead launches 4 Chaos Eater projectiles at your target. Holding it while [｢ Beelzebub, Lord of Gourmet ｣](../../../tr-nightmares/abilities/ultimate-skills/beelzebub.md) (TR: Nightmares) is mastered adds **200** damage to Beelzebub's corrosion.

A High Magisteel weapon you can hold in one or both hands. Two-handed it deals **26** attack damage at **1** attack speed; one-handed **24** damage at **0.8** speed. It also has +0.5 blocks of reach and 25% sweeping damage. Durability: **2,500**.
