## What it does

A **Dwarf village**, home to [Dwarf](../mobs/dwarf.md)s (with some goblins, lizardmen and a pegasus about), built from a plaza, roads, homes, farms and a mine. Its buildings have their own loot chests: homes, the guard post, fisherman, library, magic trainer, smithy, market, merchant stalls, farms and the **royal tower**. The royal tower's royal warp pad takes you to the arena of [Gazel Dwargo](../mobs/gazel-dwargo.md).

Generates in Slope, Cherry Grove, Meadow, Savanna Plateau, Snowy Slopes, about one every 30 chunks (at least 10 chunks apart).
