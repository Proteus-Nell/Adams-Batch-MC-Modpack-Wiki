## What it does

Blood loss. Every second it deals **0.5** blood-drain damage per level, and ignores the usual invulnerability frames after a hit. Prickly Hands and Deus Sanguis inflict it on melee hits, and the [Serrated](../enchantments/serrated.md) enchantment does too.

## Applied by

[Deus Sanguis](../abilities/unique-skills/deus-sanguis.md), [Prickly Hands](../abilities/common-skills/prickly-hands.md)
