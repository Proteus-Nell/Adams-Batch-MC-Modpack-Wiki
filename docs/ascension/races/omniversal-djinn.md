## Evolution

- **Evolves from:** [Infinite Djinn](infinite-djinn.md)

### Requirements to evolve into Omniversal Djinn

Each requirement adds its weight to the evolution progress bar; you can evolve at 100%.

| Requirement | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach Existence Points of 10,000,000 | 20% |
| Kill 4 bosses | 20% |
| Consume 50 of [Elemental Essence](../../tensura-reincarnated/items/materials/elemental-essence.md) | 20% |
| Consume 50 of [Daemon Essence](../../tensura-reincarnated/items/materials/daemon-essence.md) | 20% |
| Consume 50 of [Dragon Essence](../../tensura-reincarnated/items/materials/dragon-essence.md) | 20% |
