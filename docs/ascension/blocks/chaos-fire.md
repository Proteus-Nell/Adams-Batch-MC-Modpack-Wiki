## What it does

White fire left behind where The Imprisoned Jester's Chaos Spades hit. It can only be placed on solid ground (not on water). Anything standing in it that isn't fire-immune takes **1** spiritual (soul) damage every 10 ticks. It doesn't spread or burn blocks, and goes out on its own after 300 ticks.
