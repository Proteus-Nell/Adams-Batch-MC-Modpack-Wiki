# Mechanics

TensuraMoreSkills adds new Unique and Ultimate skills to [Tensura: Reincarnated](@/tensura-reincarnated/mechanics/index.md), plus a few races and dimensions.

## Getting the skills

Almost every skill in this mod has an **obtainment** section in [`tensuramoreskills-grand.toml`](@/tensura-more-skills/configs/config-tensuramoreskills-grand.md):

- `acquirementMagiculeCost` is the magicule cost to acquire the skill naturally.
- `requiredSkillId`, and whether that skill must be mastered, set which skill you need first. Most Ultimates evolve from a specific Unique skill.
- `acquirementMastery` is the mastery the skill starts with once acquired.

Each skill's page lists these values under **Stats (config defaults)**, together with its mode costs and cooldowns.

## Bloodfiends and Black Communion

The **Bloodfiend** race line grows through blood. A Bloodfiend can turn a willing player into kindred with *Kindred Rite*: the target sneaks and lets the Bloodfiend use the rite again to accept. A **Crimson Progenitor** can open **Black Communion**, a domain fed by blood offered at a **Blood Altar** (only Bloodfiends can feed it).

## Parasites and symbiotes

Parasite skills can perform a *Hostile Takeover* of a body. A symbiote bond has to be deep enough before a parasite can be expelled.

## Dimensions

The mod adds three dimensions: the **Asmoday Prison Realm**, the **Kingdom of Avalon** and **Paths**. See [Dimensions](@/tensura-more-skills/dimensions/index.md).

All of the mod's commands (many are skill-specific tools for operators) are on the [Commands](@/tensura-more-skills/commands/index.md) page.
