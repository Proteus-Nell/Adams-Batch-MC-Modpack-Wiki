# Corail Tombstone: what things do

Written from the mod's code and its in-game Compendium (Corail Tombstone 9.5.6). Each `## id` section becomes the "What it does" part of that entry's page.

<!-- kind: items -->

## tombstone:grave_dust
A crafting ingredient for Grave Marble, scrolls and tablets. Undead creatures drop it, cats bring it as morning gifts, and lost tablets sometimes leave some when they break.

You can also **combine it in your inventory**: right-click a stack of Grave Dust onto one of these to turn each pair into something new:

| Combine with | Result |
|---|---|
| Blue Ice | {{link:tombstone:dust_of_frost}} |
| Gunpowder | {{link:tombstone:dust_of_vanishing}} |
| Snowball | 3 {{link:tombstone:smoke_ball}} |

{{auto}}

## tombstone:dust_of_frost
An easy retreat. Use it to vanish and reappear a few steps **behind** you, leaving a wall of powder snow (about 5 blocks wide and 3 high) where you stood. The snow melts away after 10 seconds. It has a 10-second cooldown. Make it by combining Grave Dust with Blue Ice in your inventory; servers can disable it.

{{auto}}

## tombstone:essence_of_undeath
A crafting ingredient for decorative graves. Undead creatures rarely drop it, and you can also get it by fishing in deep water or from cats' morning gifts.

{{auto}}

## tombstone:impregnated_diamond
A crafting ingredient for the {{link:tombstone:receptacle_of_familiar}}.

{{auto}}

## tombstone:grave_plate
**Memorial Plaque.** Engrave a name on it in an anvil (with an iron ingot), then use it on a decorative grave to put that name on the grave. Replacing the plaque on a grave that already has one depends on the server config.

{{auto}}

## tombstone:strange_tablet
A blank tablet for making **Magic Tablets** by combining it in your inventory: Gunpowder makes a Tablet of Assistance, a Golden Carrot a Tablet of Cupidity, a Red Mushroom a Tablet of Home, Bone Meal a {{link:tombstone:tablet_of_recall}} and Bones a Tablet of Guard.

{{auto}}

## tombstone:magic_scroll
A scroll holding a potion effect. Once enchanted with a Grave Soul, hold right-click to get its effect; the scroll is used up. Ancient scrolls affect everyone around you, the others can be used on an ally too. Their duration is set in the config and grows with the Glyphographer perk.

{{auto}}

## tombstone:abandoned_grave
An old grave found in the world. Right-click it to **raid** it: you get loot (better with Luck) and some knowledge of death, and the grave crumbles. The first underwater grave you raid also gives the Coral Chant scroll, and until you've used a Ritual Flute there's a 1 in 5 chance of finding one. It can't be broken normally or blown up.

{{auto}}

## tombstone:dark_marble, tombstone:white_marble, tombstone:blue_marble, tombstone:green_marble, tombstone:carmin_marble
**Grave Marble**, the building block of decorative graves. It's very resistant to explosions. White Marble is made by adding Bone Meal to the recipe.

{{auto}}
