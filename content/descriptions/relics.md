# Relics: what things do

Written from the mod's code (Relics 0.10.7.8). Each `## id` section becomes the "What it does" part of that entry's page.

## relics:relic_experience_bottle
A throwable bottle, like a Bottle o' Enchanting, but for relics. Where it lands it bursts into 10 to 19 **relic experience orbs** (1 to 3 experience each). Picking the orbs up levels the relics you're wearing that aren't at max level yet. Dispensers can fire it too.

## relics:solid_snowball
A packed snowball made with the {{link:relics:wool_mitten}}, which can keep packing more snow into it. Throw it with right-click. If you're wearing the Wool Mitten when it hits, it deals damage and **stuns** the target, both scaling with the snowball's size and the mitten's level. Without the mitten it does nothing. It's freezing to carry: unless you wear the Wool Mitten, holding one in your inventory slowly freezes you, as if you stood in powder snow. It stacks to 1.

## relics:phantom_block
A temporary purple platform made by the {{link:relics:phantom_boot}}. You can only stand on it while wearing Phantom Boots with the ability toggled on, and only if you aren't sneaking (sneak to drop through). Landing on it hard bounces you back up. It vanishes by itself after 4 to 6 seconds, and breaks instantly.

## relics:researching_table
A decorative wooden table that gives off light level 15. In this version of Relics it has no function: nothing ever puts an item on it and right-clicking it does nothing.

## relics:anti_heal
Stops all healing while it lasts. Given by the {{link:relics:spore_sack}}'s spores and by the {{link:morerelics:wonder_of_u}}.

## relics:bleeding
Every second it deals magic damage equal to **5% of your current health** (at most 10). The {{link:relics:rage_glove}} inflicts it for 5 seconds.

## relics:confusion
Reverses your movement controls: forward walks you backward and left moves you right.

## relics:immortality
You **can't be hurt** at all while it lasts. The {{link:relics:holy_locket}} gives it (and extends it) when you kill something.

## relics:paralysis
You can't move, jump or sneak while it lasts. Mobs are frozen in place too. The {{link:relics:jellyfish_necklace}} and the {{link:morerelics:wonder_of_u}} inflict it.

## relics:stun
A stronger paralysis: you can't move, look around, attack or use items, and all your keys except Escape are ignored. Stars circle over the stunned creature's head. The {{link:relics:infinity_ham}}, thrown {{link:relics:solid_snowball}}s and the {{link:morerelics:wonder_of_u}} inflict it.

## relics:vanishing
Makes you **completely invisible**, including your armor and held items, and mobs can't see you. The {{link:relics:leafy_ring}} and {{link:relics:midnight_robe}} give it for as long as their conditions hold.
