# Mechanics

Elite Tensura ships its own in-game guide, the **Elite Tensura Codex**. Its full text is reproduced in the [Codex](@/elite-tensura/mechanics/guide/index.md) section, with chapters on Ultimate, Unique, Extra and Intrinsic skills, races, the Forge, Records, Nations, Systems and Magic.

## At a glance

- **New skills.** Ultimate skills such as Gaia, Hephaestus, Ouroboros and Beelzebuth sit alongside Unique skills and Extra skills. See [Abilities](@/elite-tensura/abilities/index.md).
- **Saiyan-style races** with deity evolutions. See [Races](@/elite-tensura/races/index.md).
- **The Forge.** A crafting and progression system for legendary gear, covered in the Codex's *Forge* chapter.
- **Nations, Records and seasons.** Server-wide systems explained in the Codex's *Nations*, *Records* and *Systems* chapters.
- A new dimension (Walpurgis Hall), a biome, blocks and bosses. See [Dimensions](@/elite-tensura/dimensions/index.md), [Blocks](@/elite-tensura/blocks/index.md) and [Mobs](@/elite-tensura/mobs/index.md).

All of the mod's balance values are on the [Configs](@/elite-tensura/configs/index.md) page.
